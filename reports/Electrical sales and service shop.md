# Serial numbers turn a khata into a service business

Grocery Khata should build "Sales & service" as a new shop type whose backbone is the **serial number**: every pump, motor, inverter, battery, AC or solar unit is captured at goods inward, printed on the sale bill with its warranty start date, and then reused by service jobs, warranty claims to the company and AMC contracts. Most of the parts already exist in the app. The furniture workshop's jobs (material, labour, other costs, advance and final bill), technician PINs, suppliers and purchase bills, the expense book and WhatsApp messages cover maybe two thirds of the workflow. What is missing is the serial and warranty register, a warranty-claim document raised against the company, AMC contracts with visit schedules, and a trip record that carries technician travel, food, stay and advances against one or more jobs. The market gap is real: billing apps such as Vyapar, myBillBook and Tally handle GST billing and stock but have no proper job cards or AMC, while field-service tools such as Zoho FSM, Mo6o and Fyond handle jobs and AMC but have weak or no khata and stock ([Vyapar comparison](https://vyaparapp.in/comparison-with-other-apps?brand=marg); [Mo6o](https://mo6o.com/amc); [Fyond](https://www.fyond.com/field-service-management-software)). A dealer who does both today runs Tally or Vyapar plus WhatsApp plus paper job cards. Build first, in order: serial-linked buying and selling, the service job with parts deducted from stock, the warranty claim with ageing, and the technician trip with advances. AMC and the AI features come next.

## The serial number decides whether the company pays

Buying in this trade happens through company or distributor channels on short credit. A pan-India dealer survey found Havells debtor days of about **15 to 30 days**, with some peers preferring channel financing to credit ([Investec dealer survey](https://www.investec.com/content/dam/india/insights/Pan-India-dealer-survey-Demand-weak-but-market-shares-intact.pdf)), and company dealer-support schemes are big enough to move a brand's margins ([Motilal Oswal](https://www.motilaloswal.com/site/rreports/HTML/636204129240926853/index.htm)). Those sources date from 2015 to 2017, and no current public data on margins, scheme slabs or price lists turned up, because companies share them privately. The purchase bill therefore needs scheme and discount lines (cash discount, target slab, and turnover rebates that arrive later as credit notes), plus a payables ageing view against the credit period. The existing supplier and purchase-bill screens can take these with modest changes.

Stock has two kinds of item. **Whole units** need serial capture at inward and are costed at actual cost per serial. **Loose spares** such as capacitors, bearings, seals, PCBs, impellers and copper winding wire are counted by quantity or by kilogram. Two more stock states matter: "defective, awaiting return to company" and "claim replacement received". Without them, a returned dud or a free replacement unit ends up in saleable stock.

Selling is where claims are won or lost. Luminous rejects battery claims when the warranty-card barcode is defaced or does not match the battery, asks for the card and the original invoice, and in one manual asks for the card within **7 days** to register the warranty ([Luminous LIFEMAX manual](https://manuals.luminousindia.com/manuals/LIFEMAX.pdf); [Luminous Eco Charge manual](https://manuals.luminousindia.com/manuals/eco-charge-wc-6963290.pdf)). Exide runs claims through the authorised retailer, using the purchase record or the serial ([Exide warranty terms](https://www.exideindustries.com/products/automotive-batteries/automotive-batteries-warranty.aspx)). Claims are commonly rejected when the invoice does not match the serial ([Laptop Repair World](https://www.laptoprepairworld.com/blog/laptop-warranty-claim-process-india/)). Each bill line should carry the serial, warranty period and start date, with installation as a separate line, and each sold serial should carry a "registered with company: yes/no, date" flag that prompts the shopkeeper until it is done. Warranty terms also create service work. Taro pumps include two free services in a 2-year warranty ([Taro](https://www.taropumps.com/blog/the-five-ticks-guarantee)). PM-KUSUM solar pump tenders bind the vendor to **5 years** of full-system warranty, repair and maintenance ([SECI PM-KUSUM RfS](https://www.seci.co.in/Upload/Tender/SECI000093-7633709-RfSunderPM-KUSUM_Component-B-Finalupload.pdf)). B2B customers (farmers who pay after harvest, contractors, housing societies) fit the existing customer khata.

## One job card carries shop repairs, field visits, claims and AMC

Service work already runs on paper: rewinding firms keep job cards, rewinding logs and test reports ([Qureos listing](https://app.qureos.com/jobs/assistant-service-manager-1757575673499)), and solar pump technicians fill in a field service report that the farmer signs ([SimplyHired](https://www.simplyhired.co.in/job/966q7EHAX7N3Iv7kCqNMXoxabBxlXb5oFSTZZ1VL4gb6Ii-hvXvD-A)). The furniture job model extends naturally. The job card should hold the customer, product and serial, complaint, an in-warranty flag looked up from the sale record, an estimate with approval, parts issued from stock, labour, and rewinding details (HP, wire gauge, copper kg), then a test result, delivery, and payment or dues. Rewinding is priced by HP band. A BHEL tender priced 8 to 12 HP submersible winding at about **Rs 5,104 per motor**, and a vendor listing quotes Rs 1,200 to 8,000 for motor rewinding ([BHEL price bid](https://tenders.bhel.com/sites/default/files/Price_Bid_OT-02-2022-04-09-12%3A14%3A39.pdf); [FatafatService](https://www.fatafatservice.com/vendor-categories/motor-and-pump-service/abdul-gafoor)), so a rate table by HP band is a useful default. Out-of-warranty parts are commonly charged at the company price list ([A. O. Smith manual](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/1313550/A-O-Smith-X8.html?page=28)). An optional "free re-repair within N days" rule, like D-Link's 30-day labour waiver, is worth supporting ([D-Link terms](https://service.dlink.co.in/resources/Dlink%20Service%20Center%20-%20Terms%20&%20Conditions.pdf)).

Field calls need a call log that records the complaint date, because response clocks carry money. Arunachal's PM-KUSUM terms give **5 days** to fix a pump, then charge Rs 500 per pump per day, rising to Rs 1,000 ([Mercom](https://mercomindia.com/arunachal-pradesh-500-solar-water-pumps)). Haryana moved to forfeit the deposits of firms with pending complaints ([Saur Energy](https://www.saurenergy.com/solar-energy-news/pm-kusum-haryana-orders-to-forfeit-deposits-of-errant-solar-firms)). Open calls should show days elapsed, and visits should close with a photo, signature or customer OTP. Mo6o leads its pitch with OTP sign-off and WhatsApp payment links ([Mo6o](https://mo6o.com/amc)).

The **warranty claim** should be its own document against the company: parts replaced free, labour or visit charges claimed, and defective parts sent back with a dispatch record, moving through submitted, approved, credited or rejected. Brand reimbursement rates and settlement delays are not public, so the app should let each shop enter its own per-brand claim rates. **AMC** needs a contract with start and end dates, fee, number of visits, an automatic visit-due list and renewal reminders on WhatsApp. Battery warranties are void if distilled-water topping is skipped ([Luminous manual](https://manuals.luminousindia.com/manuals/LIFEMAX.pdf)), which gives the dealer a natural reason to sell a maintenance AMC. Dealers have asked Tally customisers for exactly this set: warranty dates per item, AMC visits by area or route, job status and parts with serials ([Freelancer spec](https://www.freelancer.de/projects/software-architecture/tally-customization-24640314/)).

## Trips, not jobs, are where technician money leaks

Field technicians in pump, solar and battery service earn roughly **Rs 15,000 to 40,000 a month**, and travel allowance is a standard benefit ([Apna GEMS](https://www.apna.co/job/kolar/field-service-engineer-483000262); [Apna SunAP](https://apna.co/job/bengaluru-bangalore/field-service-engineer-932100727)). No public source gives DA norms or advance practice at small dealers, so the app should treat these as shop settings rather than assume rates. The design that fits the existing staff-with-PIN model is a **trip** record. A technician opens a trip and takes an advance. Fuel or km, bus and train fares, lodging and meals are logged with bill photos, and cash collected from customers on site is recorded. One or more jobs are attached to the trip. At settlement, bills and collections are netted against the advance and the balance carries forward on the technician's own ledger, in the same way as a customer khata. Trip costs are then split across the trip's jobs: equally by default, with options to split by job value or distance. Expenses not tied to any trip go to the existing expense book as overheads. KaryaFlow and Fieldproxy are the only Indian tools found that even mention technician expenses ([KaryaFlow](https://karyaflow.in/blog/top-mobile-apps-field-technician-management-india); [Fieldproxy on Techjockey](https://techjockey.com/detail/fieldproxy)), so this is a point of difference.

Profit then falls out at three levels. **Per sale**: sale value minus the unit's landed cost (purchase price net of claimable GST plus inward freight, actual cost per serial, weighted average for spares). **Per job**: labour and parts income minus parts at stock cost, technician time (salary divided by hours, or the actual per-job payout) and the job's share of trip cost. Warranty jobs should book the claim as a receivable from the company **when it is raised**. Otherwise a cash-basis shopkeeper sees every warranty job as a loss, and rejected claims should be written off against the job. AMC fees should be spread across the contract months. **Per month**: goods margin plus service margin minus overheads. No authoritative Indian guide on field-service job costing was found, so these are standard cost-accounting rules and product choices, not sourced norms. The monthly report set follows from this: sales versus service income with HSN/SAC summaries, profit by job and job type, technician-wise jobs, parts, expenses and margin, warranty claims pending by company in 0-30, 31-60 and 60+ day bands, AMC renewals and visits due, customer dues, company dues and supplier payables, serial-wise stock with parts below reorder level, and a list of **visits closed without a bill**.

## GST hinges on how the bill is split

After GST 2.0 took effect on 22 September 2025, **ACs dropped from 28% to 18%** ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-air-conditioners)), **solar modules from 12% to 5%** ([Avaada](https://www.avaada.com/?p=29666)), and motors (8501), inverters and UPS (8504) and all batteries (8507) are at 18% ([ClearTax electronics](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-on-electronics); [Tally FAQs](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/new-gst-rates-2025-key-changes-and-faqs)). Sources conflict on fans (18% versus 5%), pumps (agricultural pumps reportedly at 5%) and solar inverters ([ClearTax fans](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-on-fans); [Bajaj Finserv](https://bajajfinserv.in/gst-on-electronics); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=985076); [disytax](https://disytax.com/?p=17092)), and the official notification was not checked. HSN and rate must therefore be editable per item, with a dated rate history, and a CA should confirm the defaults. Repair, installation and AMC fall under **SAC 9987, mostly at 18%** ([Busy SAC 9987](https://busy.in/sac-code-9987)).

The bill needs four modes: goods only, labour only, goods plus installation on separate lines, and a single "supplied and installed" price. A West Bengal ruling treated AC supply-cum-installation as a composite supply taxed at the AC's rate ([WB AAR 47/2018-19](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/AAR/wb_47_2018-19_alok_bhanuka.pdf)), while installation billed separately is an 18% service ([Busy](https://busy.in/hsn/installation-hsn-code/)). The default should be separate lines, with a warning when a 5% solar module is bundled with installation, because that treatment is disputed. For warranty, **Circular 195/07/2023** says free warranty parts and repairs carry no GST and need no ITC reversal, any extra charged to the customer is taxable, and an invoice the dealer raises on the manufacturer for parts or labour is a taxable supply ([CBIC circular](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-06/circular-cgst-195.pdf)). Circular 216/10/2024 extends this to whole-unit replacement and treats separately sold extended warranty as taxable ([VJM Global](https://www.vjmglobal.com/ja/blog/gst-liability-and-input-tax-credit-availability-in-cases-involving-warranty-extended-warranty-clarification-in-furtherance-to-circular)). In practice the customer gets a zero bill or delivery challan, the parts leave stock without reversal, and the claim to the company is a GST invoice. Whether a dealer's own goodwill replacement, without company backing, gets the same treatment is unclear.

On income tax, the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026 and gathers TDS into section 393. Contractor TDS (old 194C) stays at **1% or 2%**, and purchase TDS (old 194Q) at **0.1% above Rs 50 lakh**, deducted only by buyers with turnover above Rs 10 crore ([Busy](https://busy.in/tds/section-194c-contractors-subcontractors/); [StudyCafe](https://studycafe.in/tds-rate-chart-for-fy-2026-2027-under-income-tax-act-2025-420727.html)), so most small dealers are on the receiving end rather than deducting it. The app should record TDS deducted by companies on claim payments, or by B2B customers, as a receivable to match against AIS, and warn when technician payouts approach contractor-TDS limits for shops under audit. Trading plus repair counts as a business, not a profession, so presumptive taxation of the 44AD kind is the relevant comparison. Showing presumptive income next to actual profit is useful, but the renumbered sections and thresholds under the 2025 Act were not confirmed from primary text.

## AI earns its place on voice notes and leakage checks

AI summaries of technician notes are now shipping in Western field-service tools. BlueFolder added them in July 2026, and ServiceBrief AI turns calls and notes into jobs and service reports for electrical and HVAC trades ([BlueFolder news](https://www.webull.com/news/15267215536317440); [ServiceBrief AI](https://www.producthunt.com/products/servicebrief-ai)). Practitioners advise fixed output fields (outcome, parts used, follow-ups, warranty flag) because free-form summaries come out uneven ([ContractorAIHub](https://contractoraihub-com.pages.dev/guides/technician-voice-notes-to-job-summaries-with-ai.html)). No independent accuracy benchmarks exist for AI fault diagnosis ([DGUV blog](https://werde.kulturprofi.dguv.de/blog/posts/when-speech-becomes-a-service-ticket-how-speaksphere-rethinks-industrial-field-service-documentation/)). For Grocery Khata the strongest uses are, first, a Hindi or Hinglish voice note that fills the job card's fixed fields and proposes parts for the technician to confirm before stock is deducted. Second, reading photos of company price lists and purchase bills into items and rates, which fits a trade where price lists arrive as private PDFs. Third, suggesting likely parts from the shop's own job history. Fourth, flags for closed jobs with no bill, warranty claims nearing rejection age, and trip expenses well above the shop's usual for the distance. No Indian SME tool was found advertising price-list reading or leakage flags, which suggests a gap, though the search was limited. AI should always propose and the shopkeeper confirm, especially for stock and GST entries.

On pricing, Indian field-service tools run from about **Rs 3,500 a year** (Fyond) to **Rs 1,999 to 9,999 a month** (Mo6o), and Zoho FSM partners quote about Rs 1,000 to 1,800 per user per month ([Fyond](https://www.fyond.com/field-service-management-software); [Mo6o](https://mo6o.com/amc); [PrecisionTech](https://precisiontech.in/software/zoho/zoho-fsm/)). Signals from vendors point to WhatsApp-first communication and offline use at sites with poor signal ([Quickestimate](https://quickestimate.co/blog/red-flags-when-choosing-solar-crm)).

| Phase | Build | Reuses |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shop type, serial capture at inward and on bill, warranty start and period, registration flag, defective and replacement stock states, four bill modes with HSN/SAC | Stock, purchase bills, bills |
| 2 | Service job card with serial lookup, parts from stock, HP-band labour rates, OTP or photo closure, WhatsApp job status | Furniture jobs, advance and final bill, WhatsApp |
| 3 | Warranty claim document, claim GST invoice, accrued receivable, ageing report | Suppliers as companies |

## Conclusion

The main finding is that in this trade the money is lost in paperwork, not in stocking. A rejected claim over a mismatched serial, a field visit closed without a bill, technician cash that is never settled, or a pending complaint that runs into penalty days each costs more than a slightly wrong price. That changes what "done first" means: the serial register and the claim ageing list are revenue features, not admin. Grocery Khata's advantage is that it already holds the khata, stock and GST side that field-service tools lack, so tying a job to a serial, a stock issue, a trip and a claim in one record is something no competitor found here does.

Two things need checking before launch. A CA should confirm the disputed GST rates (fans, pumps, solar inverters, bundled solar installation) and the 2025 Act's presumptive and TDS sections. A handful of real dealers should be interviewed on brand claim rates, settlement delays and DA practice, since none of these are public and they decide whether warranty work is profitable at all.

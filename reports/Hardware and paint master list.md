# Seed 550 editable rows for paint and hardware

Grocery Khata should seed roughly **550 master rows across the paint and hardware shop types**: about **300 paint rows** (around 200 brand product lines across Asian Paints, Birla Opus, Birla White, Berger, Nerolac, Dulux, Nippon, Indigo, JSW, Shalimar and British Paints, plus about 100 accessory, thinner, putty, adhesive and waterproofing rows) and about **250 hardware rows** (fasteners, door and furniture fittings, locks, hand and power tools, adhesives, plumbing, and the Tata steel, wire, sheet, pipe and tool lines). Each row should be a product line with a pack ladder (1/4/10/20 L for emulsions, 50 ml to 4 L for enamels, 1/5/20/40 kg for putty), not one row per pack, because tinted shades share a base price and billing happens per pack. The tax picture is simpler than the notes make it look: **every paint, primer, putty, adhesive and most hardware heading sits at 18% today**, whatever the history. Only a handful of headings need a "check with CA" flag: paint brushes (9603, 5% or 18%), farm hand tools (8201, now reported at 5%), POP (2520), asbestos-cement sheets (6811) and waterproofing chemicals (3824). Prices are the weak link. Fewer than one in five paint rows has a sourced price, the aggregator figures disagree by up to 2x, and no hardware item price was sourced at all. Every price must therefore ship as an **editable default marked "verify with dealer list"**, or as blank, never as a fact.

## Paint brands follow one four-tier ladder, which should drive the categories

Every major brand sells the same shape of range: a luxury, premium, regular and economy tier for interior emulsion, the same for exterior, plus enamels, primers, putty, wood finishes and waterproofing. That makes **category = finish type, line = brand tier** the right model, with roughly ten categories per paint shop: interior emulsion, exterior emulsion, distemper, primer, putty, enamel and metal finish, wood finish, waterproofing, texture, stainer and tinter, thinner, and tools.

**Asian Paints** is the largest block, at about 150 candidate rows. Interior runs Royale (luxury), Apcolite (premium), Tractor (economy) and Ace. Exterior runs Apex Ultima, Apex Weatherproof and Ace Exterior. Then come the Trucare primers and putty, SmartCare waterproofing, Apcolite enamels and Woodtech wood finishes. Aggregator 20 L prices give usable anchors: **Royale Luxury about Rs 15,280, Apex Ultima Rs 12,710, Apcolite Premium Emulsion Rs 8,750, Tractor Emulsion Rs 4,240** ([Aapka Painter](https://aapkapainter.com/paints/asian-paints-prices/emulsion)). Primers have full ladders, for example Trucare Interior Wall Primer WT at **133 / 486 / 1,128 / 2,104** for 1/4/10/20 L ([Aapka Painter primers](https://aapkapainter.com/paints/asian-paints-prices/primer)). The only official MRP found was **Apcolite Premium Gloss Enamel 1 L at Rs 412** ([asianpaints.com](https://www.asianpaints.com/content/ap/en/home/products/wood-and-metals/metal-enamels/apcolite-premium-gloss-enamel.html)). Retail listings for the same tin range up to Rs 622 ([Moglix](https://www.moglix.com/asian-paints-apcolite-premium-1-litre-gloss-enamel-eb1/mp/msn8580rgzyl92)), which shows how loose "typical price" is. The same aggregator's own FAQ quotes Apex Ultima 20 L at Rs 1,500 to 1,800, contradicting its own list. Its Tractor Emulsion Advanced figure of Rs 2,147 for 20 L also looks wrong next to plain Tractor's Rs 4,240.

**Birla Opus** names come straight from the brand's site and should be used exactly as written. The luxury tier is One (One Pure for interior, One True for exterior), then Calista (premium), Style (economy) and Dream. Allied lines are Calista Sparkle (enamels), Allwood (wood), Alldry (waterproofing) and One Pro / Calista Pro / Style Pro (primer and putty) ([Birla Opus interiors](https://www.birlaopus.com/shop-wall-paint/interiors); [Birla Opus exteriors](https://www.birlaopus.com/shop-wall-paint/exteriors)). Names like "Kids", "One Weathershield" or Calista Ever Wash as an exterior are wrong. Ever Wash is an interior line. Price anchors for 20 L are **One True Vision Rs 17,750, One Pure Elegance Rs 14,240, Calista Neo Star Rs 9,886, Calista Ever Wash Rs 8,190, Style Colour Smart Rs 4,670** ([Aapka Painter Birla Opus](https://aapkapainter.com/products/paints/birla-opus-price)). A NoBroker blog adds enamel price bands, but it also invents product names such as "Opus Weather King" ([NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/birla-opus-paint-price/)). Take nothing from it except, cautiously, the price bands.

**Birla White** belongs mostly in the putty and cement group: WallCare, WallSeal waterproof, Zero Lime, PrePaint and Scented Rose putties, Textura RF and TF textures, Levelplast, primer, distemper, tile adhesive and white cement ([UltraTech Birla White](https://www.ultratechcement.com/products/birla-white-cement); [LnT SuFin](https://lntsufin.com/product/birla-white-wallseal-waterproof-wall-putty-20-kg/17163-4314)). **WallCare 40 kg trades at Rs 550 to 730** a bag ([IndiaMART](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/birla-wall-putty.html)).

**Berger** maps cleanly onto the tiers. Interior is Silk / Silk Glamor / Silk Breathe Easy, then Easy Clean, Rangoli and Bison. Exterior is WeatherCoat (Anti Dustt, Long Life, All Guard) and Walmasta. Enamels are Luxol, Butterfly and Jadoo. Wood is Imperia and Woodkeeper. Waterproofing is HomeShield, Dampstop, Seal-O-Prime and Dampshield ([Coatings World](https://www.coatingsworld.com/heaps/view/7401/4/341639)). Berger prices are the least reliable of the big three. WeatherCoat Long Life at Rs 11,360 is quoted as 10 L on one page and 20 L on another, and Easy Clean ranges from Rs 5,000 to 6,725 depending on the page ([NoBroker 20 L](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/berger-paint-20-litre-price/); [NoBroker per litre](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/berger-paint-per-litre-price/)). The only official Berger MRP list dates from 2018 ([Berger PDF](https://old.bergerpaints.com/resources/pdf/gst/mrp-dec18.pdf)). Seed Berger with names and packs, and leave most prices blank.

**Other brands** need about 55 rows. Nerolac: Impressions, Beauty, Excel, Suraksha and enamel. Dulux: Velvet Touch, SuperClean, Promise, Weathershield and SuperCover. Nippon: Atomos, Spotless, Matex, Weatherbond and Satin Glo. Indigo: Floor Coat, Tile Coat, Metallic and Ceiling. JSW: Halo, Aura and Vogue. Shalimar: Superlac and No.1 Silk. British Paints: Bonus. Add generic red oxide, zinc chromate, wood primer, aluminium paint, black japan and cement paint. Only Nerolac has any price evidence, and that conflicts too: **Impressions Eco Clean 20 L is Rs 8,103 on one aggregator and Rs 11,069 on another** ([Aapka Painter Nerolac](https://aapkapainter.com/products/paints/nerolac-paints-price); [NoBroker Nerolac](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/nerolac-paints-price-list/)). Sub-line names for Dulux, Nippon, Indigo, JSW, Shalimar and British Paints come from catalogue knowledge, not page checks. Mark them "verify" and let admins prune any that have been renamed.

Accessories turn over faster than tins and need about 100 rows. These cover brushes by width (1 to 6 inch), rollers and sleeves (4/7/9 inch), trays, poles, sandpaper by grit (60 to 1200), masking tape, putty blades, thinners (NC, enamel/MTO, PU, turpentine), stainers, white cement, POP, M-Seal, Fevicol and Fevikwik, Dr. Fixit waterproofing, silicone, polish and varnish, spray cans and tile adhesive. Even the official Dr. Fixit page contradicts itself, showing **Pidiproof LW+ 200 ml at Rs 37 in one place and Rs 47 in another** ([Dr. Fixit](https://www.drfixit.co.in/product/new/dr.-fixit-lw--180.html)).

## The GST conflict changes nothing about today's rate

The notes split on history. One camp says paints under 3208/3209 **moved from 28% to 18% on 22 September 2025** ([Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/hsn-code-paint); [Gimbooks](https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/paint-hsn-code-3208-gst-rate/amp/)). The other says paints were cut to 18% back in 2018 and the GST 2.0 changes left them untouched ([Busy](https://busy.in/gst-rates/paints.md); [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/paint-hsn-code-and-gst-rate)). ClearTax is cited by both camps. The 2018 account is the more credible one. The July 2018 GST Council cut is widely reported to have moved paints and varnishes from 28% to 18%, and the 2025 headlines on building materials centred on cement. Even so, no CBIC notification text was fetched in any pass, so this remains **unconfirmed**. It matters for one thing only: whether bills dated before 22 September 2025 should have carried 28%. For seeding, every source agrees on the answer: **3208, 3209 and 3210 are at 18% now**.

The genuine open items are smaller. Wall putty (3214) is 18% in most sources, but busy.in lists 12% and one lookup shows 5% or 18% for 32141000 ([busy.in](https://busy.in/gst-rates/paints.md); [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/hsn-code/32141000)). Use 18%. One aggregator shows a 5%/18% split under heading 3208 citing Notification 9/2025 ([TaxClue](https://taxclue.in/hsn-code/3208)). Nothing corroborates it, so ignore it but log it. **Cement, including white cement 2523, did move from 28% to 18%** on 22 September 2025, and that is well sourced ([Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/cement-gst-percentage-rate-hsn-code-applicability/); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/economy/news/gst-rate-cut-medium-term-gains-cement-firms-125090401223_1.html)). Paint brushes under 9603 are the real ambiguity. The "brooms, brushes" line shows 5%, but other sub-lines differ ([Busy 9603](https://busy.in/hsn/paint-brush-hsn-code.md); [ClearTax 9603](https://cleartax.in/s/brooms-brushes-floor-sweepers-mops-feather-dusters-gst-rates-hsn-code-9603)).

On the hardware side, plastic pipes and fittings (3917) are **confirmed at 18%** ([Tally HSN 3917](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/hsn-code-3917-product-classification-gst-rate-business-filing-guide/)). So are adhesives (3506) ([KnowYourGST](https://www.knowyourgst.com/hsn-gst-tax-rate/adhesives)) and abrasive paper (6805) ([IncorpX](https://www.incorpx.io/hsn-codes/code-68053000)). Iron and steel rods and wire stay at 18% ([Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/gst-rate-construction-building-materials/)). One site claims TMT fell to 5% ([Disytax](https://disytax.com/hsn-73-articles-iron-steel-gst-rate/)), but nothing corroborates it. Agricultural hand tools are on the 5% list, while workshop tools stay at 18% ([Busy construction](https://busy.in/gst-rates/construction-materials)). That splits Tata Agrico's catalogue: the phawda, belcha, gaiti, khurpi and kulhadi go at 5%, while its hammers, pliers, spanners and files go at 18%.

## Hardware stocks five clusters, and Tata sells across all of them

A hardware master list breaks into fasteners (screws, nut-bolts, anchors, gitti, nails, hooks, binding wire), door and furniture fittings (hinges, tower bolts, aldrops, handles, channels, closers, castors), locks (padlocks, mortise, night latch, cupboard), tools (hand tools, power tools and their bits and wheels) and plumbing (PVC/UPVC/CPVC/SWR pipe and fittings, valves, taps, hoses, sanitary spares). Adhesives and building steel sit alongside. The notes draft about 210 core rows and 105 Tata and building rows, with heavy overlap, such as nails, nut-bolts and hinges appearing in both. After de-duplication, about **250 hardware rows** remain. Brands named per row (Taparia, Stanley, Godrej, Dorset, Hettich, Ebco, Supreme, Astral, Pidilite, Bosch) are trade knowledge, as are all the price bands. **No hardware item price was sourced**, so seed price as blank or as a band in the notes field, not as a selling price.

Tata's retail brands are confirmed in its annual reports. **Tiscon** is TMT rebar, **Shaktee** is galvanised roofing sheet, **Wiron** is galvanised wire, **Agrico** is hand and farm tools (including files, pliers, spanners and screwdrivers), **Structura** is structural tube and **Pravesh** is steel doors and windows ([Tata Steel AR 2014-15](https://www.tatasteel.com/investors/annual-report-2014-15/html/products-and-brands.html); [Tata Steel IR 2016-17](https://www.tatasteel.com/investors/integrated-report-2016-17/principal-activities-and-revenue-streams.html); [Tata Steel IR 2021-22](https://www.tatasteel.com/investors/integrated-report-2021-22/product-portfolio.html)). Durashine colour sheet is a Tata BlueScope product ([MGS Architecture](https://www.mgsarchitecture.in/updates/news/2342-durashine-from-tata-bluescope-steel-is-india-s-most-trusted-brand-2019.html)). Its ownership after restructuring, and any current "Tata Pipes" branding, are unconfirmed. Tiscon is the only Tata line with price evidence: **about Rs 63 to 81 per kg** for Fe500D/550SD ([Comaron](https://www.comaron.com/blog/best-tmt-bar-brand-india-2026-fe500d-comparison-price-quality)), with modelled city figures of Rs 64 to 74 ([Infralens](https://infralens.in/prices/steel)). Chennai traded at Rs 57 to 73 for all brands in June 2026 ([Tata nexarc](https://www.tatanexarc.com/steel/tmt-bars/tmt-bars-in-tamil-nadu/chennai-price/)). Steel prices move weekly, so TMT should be priced per kg and edited daily, never fixed.

Shopkeeper names matter more here than in paint, because counter staff type them. Rows should carry the trade name first and the English name as an alias: **sariya** (TMT bar, by 8/10/12/16/20/25 mm), **bandhai taar** (binding wire), **kaante wala taar** (barbed wire), **jaali** (chain link), **kantila/keel** (wire nails), **tin chaadar** (GC sheet), **cement chaadar** (AC sheet), **patti** (MS flat), **gaarder** (beam), **chaukhat** (door frame), **phawda, belcha, gaiti, kudal, khurpi, kulhadi, saabal, ghan, hathoda, plaas, pana, pechkas, reti, karni, tasla/ghamela**, **kabja** (hinge), **kunda** (hasp), **taala** (padlock) and **gitti** (rawl plug). Units are mixed and must be per row: kg for TMT, wire, nails and loose fasteners; length (3 or 6 m) for pipe; sheet or running foot for roofing; pair for hinges and channels; box of 100 for screws; piece for everything else.

That totals about **605 rows before de-duplication** and about **550 after** merging the overlaps between the accessory, adhesive and tool rows of the two trades. If the seed must be smaller at launch, the best first cut is roughly **200 rows**: the top two tiers of each of the big three paint brands, the generic primers, thinners and tools, and the hardware fasteners, hinges, locks, PVC fittings and Tiscon/Wiron rows. The long tail of luxury textures, Woodtech kits and brand sub-lines can wait for the admin CSV import.

Every row should carry name (shopkeeper form), aliases, brand, line, category, pack ladder, unit, default price per pack (nullable), MRP flag, HSN, GST and a `price_source` value of "aggregator", "official" or "none". The app should then show **"suggested price, check your dealer list"** until the shopkeeper saves their own. Two pricing rules come out of the notes. First, a tinted shade costs the base price plus a tint charge, so shades should not become separate rows. Second, smaller packs can be estimated from the 20 L price (about 1/17 to 1/19 for 1 L, 1/4.6 for 4 L, 1/2 for 10 L), but that ratio is an inference and should be labelled as one. Never seed from the NoBroker Asian Paints list, which names products that do not exist, such as "Spa Guard Tough" ([NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/asian-paints-price-list/)).

## Conclusion

The hard part of this master list is not the names or the tax. Names are stable and largely confirmed from brand sites, and tax converges on 18% with a short, known list of exceptions. The hard part is price, and that changes the product design. Grocery Khata should treat the catalogue as a **name, pack and tax authority** and treat price as the shop's own data. The app can then turn the gap into a feature. A shop that edits a Royale 20 L price is telling the platform the local dealer price, and those edits, aggregated anonymously per city, would become a better price reference than any aggregator the research found.

Before launch, one check is worth more than any extra row: pull the text of CBIC Notification 9/2025-CT(Rate) and confirm the rates for 9603, 2520, 3824, 6811, 8201 and 8301/8302. That would settle the open GST flags in one pass and close the 2018-versus-2025 question for back-dated bills.

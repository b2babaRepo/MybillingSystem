# Grocery Khata already ships most jewellery pillars

Most of the four-pillar strategy is already in the code. All jewellery work up to the girvi compounding and gram-scheme commits is merged in PR #6. Nothing jewellery-related is uncommitted. Pillar 1 is mostly done: plan items 1–8 all shipped in commit 35b6c1d, silver HUID with IS 2112:2025 grades works, standalone URD vouchers exist, and jewellery bills hard-block cash of ₹2 lakh or more and require PAN or Form 60. Pillar 3 is mostly done too: gram schemes and compound girvi exist, but the girvi "loss warning" does not. Pillar 2 is partly done: a rate message and a "Due today" list with WhatsApp buttons exist, but the rate card is text, not an image, and there is no morning push. Pillar 4 is partly done: Hindi, English and Gujarati and a Web Serial scale exist, but there is no jewellery plan gating or phone scale path. The bigger risk is the strategy text, not the code. Several of its claims are wrong or could not be found. "SwarnaVyapar" does not exist as a product, the "100 tags / 5 girvi" free-tier caps could not be found, the QR cannot open BIS CARE with the HUID filled in, the HUID is alphanumeric rather than "6-digit", and the old-gold cash rule that binds the jeweller is s.40A(3), not s.269ST. About **12–16 developer days** closes the remaining gaps. The order is: loss warning, rate-card image, honest QR wording, morning push, scheme 365-day guard, girvi cash checks, then plan gating.

## Five strategy claims need correcting before they reach marketing

**The QR claim is the most important fix.** BIS CARE's "Verify HUID" asks the customer to *type* the HUID. The app's QR scanner reads BIS licence and registration documents, not jewellery HUIDs ([BIS blog](https://www.services.bis.gov.in/php/BIS_2.0/BISBlog/bis-care-app/); [BIS apps page](https://www.bis.gov.in/bis-apps/)). No official deep link or QR payload with the HUID pre-filled was found. The HUID is a **6-character alphanumeric** code, not 6 digits ([BIS FAQ](https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/hallmarking-faqs/hallmarking-faq)). The current bill caption, "for the customer to scan and check in the BIS CARE app", is therefore misleading. Safe wording is "Check this HUID in the government BIS CARE app". A tag QR can open the shop's *own* piece page (HUID, weight, purity), but only if that page says clearly that it is not BIS and does not use BIS branding.

**Silver HUID is real, but silver hallmarking is voluntary.** Since 1 Sept 2025, silver that is hallmarked must carry a HUID under IS 2112:2025 ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/investment/story/silver-jewellery-gets-safer-govt-makes-huid-compulsory-for-hallmarked-silver-boosting-transparency-consumer-trust-505408-2025-12-06)). In June 2026, BIS said it had no timeline for making silver hallmarking mandatory ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/news/story/will-silver-hallmarking-become-mandatory-bis-assesses-readiness-of-indian-market-534988-2026-06-05)). The app should warn, not block, when a silver HUID is missing, which matches the plan.

**s.269ST is the wrong rule for old-gold payouts.** s.269ST limits the person who *receives* ₹2 lakh or more in cash ([Income Tax Dept](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-269st-8)). When the jeweller pays cash for old gold, the seller is the receiver. The rule that hits the jeweller is **s.40A(3)**: a cash payment above ₹10,000 to one person in a day is disallowed as a deduction ([Income Tax Dept](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/mode-of-receipts-and-payments)). The code already gets this right with its ₹10,000 warning on vouchers and its 269ST block on sale receipts. The marketing should say so too. From 1 April 2026, the Income-tax Act 2025 applies; secondary sources map 269ST to **s.186** ([TaxClue](https://www.taxclue.in/blog/section-269ss-269st-income-tax-act-2025-mapping/)). On PAN, Rule 114B has become **Rule 159**, and **Form 97** replaces Form 60, with some thresholds revised ([Khaitan & Co](https://www.khaitanco.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/ERGO%20-%20Decoding%20PAN%20ammendments%20-%2013%20April%202026.pdf)). The ₹2 lakh jewellery threshold under the new Rule 159 was not confirmed, so it should become a server setting with dual labels such as "Rule 114B / Rule 159". Separately, PMLA makes jewellers reporting entities for cash dealings of ₹10 lakh or more with a customer ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/finance/dealers-precious-stones-real-estate-agents-notified-pmla-act-2002.html)). That suggests a monitor as a later item.

**Competitor claims are mostly unverified.** No product called "SwarnaVyapar" was found; the nearest names are SwarnApp, from ₹9,970 ([Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/swarnapp)), and the SwarnaPro calculator ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757679306)). No rival free tier capped at "100 tags / 5 girvi" was found. GehnaERP is quote-only with no free plan ([SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/gehna-erp/pricing)). Omunim is not purely desktop any more: it markets phone and tablet use, cloud storage, a maintenance fee and free training ([omunim.com](https://www.omunim.com)). Marg is a desktop licence, ₹10,300–26,000 plus GST ([Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/marg-jewellery-software)), with yearly ARC of ₹3,000–7,200 ([Fitgap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/044588/marg-jewelry-software)). Omunim does advertise a "loss loan alert" sent "three months in advance", but it does not say what triggers it ([omunim.com](https://www.omunim.com)). The pitch should therefore say "we show the trigger", not "they lack it". No rival with a one-tap branded rate-card image was found, so this is a real but small gap.

**Girvi and scheme rules are state and statute risks, not RBI ones.** The RBI's 2025 gold-loan Directions, with LTV of 85% up to ₹2.5 lakh, 80% for ₹2.5–5 lakh and 75% above, bind only banks and NBFCs ([RBI Directions](https://website.rbi.org.in/documents/d/rbi/lendinggoldsilvercollateraldirections2025_01102025-1-)). The app can use these tiers as a labelled "RBI benchmark" default, not as a legal limit. Jeweller-pawnbrokers fall under state Acts. Tamil Nadu caps interest at a notified rate of at most the RBI bank rate plus 5% and requires an advertised auction ([TN Act](https://www.cra.tn.gov.in/tnscs/Files/act/003a_The%20Tamil%20Nadu%20Pawn%20Brokers%20Act%201943.pdf)). Karnataka caps it at 9% a year or a notified rate ([India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7163/1/13_of_1962_%28e%29.pdf)). Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, UP and Delhi caps were not verified. For schemes, money is excluded from "deposit" status only as an advance adjusted against goods within **365 days**, and BUDS 2019 reaches proprietors too ([LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/articles/law-relating-to-jewellery-brands-gold-savings-schemes-279580)). A hard tenure guard is therefore a compliance item, not a nicety.

## Three owner decisions with recommended defaults

**Pricing model.** The recommended default is a published flat yearly price with no tag or girvi caps, for example a jewellery tier priced below Marg's entry licence plus ARC. That keeps "transparent, uncapped" honest. Rivals rely on licence plus AMC/ARC or quote-only pricing. The usage cost of WhatsApp Business API reminders is small: about ₹0.115 per utility message from Oct 2026 ([TelecomTalk](https://telecomtalk.info/whatsappbusiness-price-reveal-telegram-arattai-may-benefit/1012403/)), or roughly ₹58 for 500 reminders. Start with free wa.me taps and add API sending later as an add-on. The exact rupee figure is the owner's call. The research found no reliable benchmark below the listing-site prices.

**First target state for pawnbroker rules.** The recommended default is **Gujarat**, because the UI already ships Gujarati and its Money Lenders Act 2011 applies to private lenders ([SC judgment](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2010/1744/1744_2010_10_1501_35747_Judgement_10-May-2022.pdf)). Its notified rate and notice period are unverified, though, so pay for one local CA or lawyer review before enforcing anything. If there is no pilot shop in Gujarat, **Tamil Nadu** or **Karnataka** are the safest technical starts, because their caps were verified from primary text. In every state, ship rules as warnings, not blocks.

**Counter language.** The recommended default is a **Hindi UI with English numbers and units**, with a per-customer reminder language (Hindi or English) defaulting to the shop's language. No survey of jeweller language preference was found. Omunim's six languages show demand but not preference ([omunim.com](https://www.omunim.com)). Five shop interviews would settle this more cheaply than further desk research.

## Conclusion

The strategy reads as a build plan, but for Grocery Khata it is mostly a marketing and correctness problem. The features exist. What would hurt is shipping claims that a CA or a BIS-aware customer can disprove: "scan to verify on BIS", "6-digit HUID", "s.269ST on old gold", and rivals' unverifiable free-tier caps. Fixing that wording costs under a day and removes the largest trust risk.

The one real product gap with competitive weight is the girvi loss warning. Pledged gold is revalued against rising debt every day, and Omunim advertises an alert without explaining its trigger. A visible, explained margin percentage on the Today panel, built on fine weight × today's rate and labelled as an RBI benchmark, gives a counter demo that a rival's opaque alert cannot match. Pair it with the rate-card image and the sales story has something to show in its first minute.

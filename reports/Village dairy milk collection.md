# Build the dairy centre around reconciliation

Grocery Khata should add the dairy milk collection centre as a shop type whose core is a **shift-wise collection ledger (farmer, cow/buffalo/mixed, litres or kg, fat, SNF/CLR, rate, amount)** priced by a **configurable, date-versioned rate plan**, with a WhatsApp slip per entry, a 10-day or monthly farmer bill that nets off cattle feed and advances, and a dispatch ledger that reconciles what the centre bought against what Sudha or another dairy accepted. Small Android apps such as Hamari Dairy and MCS already do fat/SNF rate charts, shifts, 10-day bills, advances and SMS, so parity alone will not win; the gap is that **no product found combines fat/SNF collection with a WhatsApp-first khata, UPI and union-bill reconciliation**, and general khata apps like OkCredit have no notion of litres, fat or rate per litre. The first release should therefore be manual-entry collection, the rate engine, slips, farmer bills with deductions, and a simple dispatch-versus-union reconciliation; device integration, OCR of analyser slips, a door-to-door "sell to homes" mode and tax extras come later. The biggest uncertainty is Bihar-specific: no official COMFED rate chart, commission per litre or payment cycle was found, and Bihar now pays many cooperative farmers directly by DBT, which changes what the centre's own books must do. That makes the product strongest for private centres and middlemen, who handle most of Bihar's marketed milk, and a record-keeping and audit tool for cooperative societies.

## Twice-daily collection defines the core data model

Village centres take milk morning and evening at fixed times; each lot is measured on the spot and sampled for fat, and the farmer gets a slip ([Amul](https://Amul.com/files/pdf/6-10-15-Cow-to-consumer.pdf)). Bihar's COMFED runs this through **32,244 village societies with 14.85 lakh members**, collecting twice a day for district union plants ([Govt of Bihar](https://betastate.bihar.gov.in/Comfed)). Where a society has an automatic milk collection unit, the unit identifies the farmer by RFID, card or barcode, weighs and analyses the milk, computes the bill and prints a thermal slip ([Prompt DPMCU](https://www.promptdairytech.com/dpmcu-milk-collection-system/)); such a station handles 120 to 150 samples an hour ([Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/agriculture/livestock/model-bankable-projects/automatic-milk-collection-units)). Scale matters for design: Amul's 13,141 societies collect about 10.16 million litres a day ([Amul AMCS](https://www.amuldairy.com/assets/img/pdf/AMCS_June_2021.pdf)), roughly 770 litres per society, so a busy centre makes a few hundred entries per shift and the entry screen must be fast enough for a queue.

Each collection entry needs date, shift, farmer, milk type, quantity with its unit, fat, SNF or CLR, rate and amount. The entry should accept SNF directly or derive it from the lactometer reading, but **the formula constants must be configurable** because sources disagree: one gives SNF = CLR/4 + 0.21 × fat + 0.36 ([Terra Insight](https://www.terra-insight.com/insights/dairy-reconciliation-fat-snf-milk-procurement-india)), another CLR/4 + 0.25 × fat + 0.6 ([IIT Kanpur virtual lab](https://dt-iitk.vlabs.ac.in/exp/snf-content-milk/procedure.html)). Litre-to-kg conversion (often taken as about 1.03) was not confirmed from a source, so the plan should store whether the buyer pays per litre or per kg and the density used. Separate cow and buffalo rates are standard in the apps reviewed, and FSSAI minimums differ by species: cow milk 3.2% fat and 8.3% SNF ([foodsafetymantra](https://foodsafetymantra.com/regulatory-insight/consumer-products/milk-cream/fssai-categorization-of-milk-products-and-their-standards)), and buffalo milk in Bihar historically 6.0/9.0, revised to 5.0/9.0, though whether that revision is in force is unclear ([dairynews7x7](https://dairynews7x7.com/news/buffalo-milk-standards-revised-to-5-fat-and-9-snf-by-fssai)). Below-standard milk is penalised or rejected under union rules ([Terra Insight](https://www.terra-insight.com/insights/dairy-reconciliation-fat-snf-milk-procurement-india)), so each entry also needs an accepted/rejected/reduced-rate status with a reason.

## A versioned rate engine has to cover five pricing methods

Indian organised dairies mostly pay two-axis prices: fat kg × fat rate plus SNF kg × SNF rate, with SNF valued at about two-thirds of fat; alternatives are fat-only, total-solids at one rate, and a flat rate by species, each with known distortions such as undervaluing cow milk or inviting cow milk to be passed off as buffalo ([agriculture.institute](https://agriculture.institute/milk-production-and-quality/how-milk-is-priced-dairy-payment)). Unions also issue fat × SNF grids in 0.1 steps, and dairy ERPs store these grids with an effective date ([Ecosire ERPNext dairy](https://ecosire.com/ja/apps/erpnext/erpnext-dairy-management)). Sudha's own spread shows it prices by quality: on 28 May 2026 the lowest grade rose **from Rs 33 to Rs 34.75 per litre and the top "SNF 10" grade from Rs 77.70 to Rs 81** ([Rashtriya Ujala](https://www.rashtriyaujala.com/2026/05/28/big-relief-for-cattle-farmers-in-bihar-sudha-dairy-hikes-milk-procurement-rate-by-over-%E2%82%B93/)), and a November 2026 DBT payout of **Rs 68.51 crore for 14.67 million litres averaged about Rs 46.71 per litre** across 106,063 producers ([eDairyNews](https://in.edairynews.com/bihar-transfers-%E2%82%B968-51-crore-directly-to-106063-dairy-farmers-and-launches-digital-control-centre-for-sudha-milk/)). Amul unions quote per kg of fat instead, for example Rs 855 to 865 for buffalo from June 2025, with a year-end final price of Rs 1,028 ([dairynews7x7](https://dairynews7x7.com/news/amul-boosts-milk-price-cuts-feed-cost-to-aid-farmers)).

The practical design is one "rate plan" object with a method (grid, two-axis on kg or litre basis, fat-only, total solids, flat), a per-species override, a below-standard rule, optional bonus or incentive lines, and an effective-from date so past entries keep their original rate. **The grid is the safest default** because any union chart can be typed or imported into it, and Prompt's Milk Sangrah already imports Excel charts on fat, fat-SNF, fat-LR and solids bases ([Milk Sangrah](https://www.promptdairytech.com/milk-sangrah-app)), so Excel import belongs in the first release. A centre needs two plans at once: the rate it pays farmers and the rate the dairy pays it, which is what makes margin and reconciliation computable. Rates change once or twice a year, usually alongside retail hikes around April to June, and seasonal premiums of up to 30% appear in the literature ([agriculture.institute](https://agriculture.institute/milk-production-and-quality/how-milk-is-priced-dairy-payment)). Year-end bonuses distributed in proportion to milk supplied are common in cooperatives; COMFED says it pays bonuses and incentives but no per-litre figure was found ([Govt of Bihar](https://betastate.bihar.gov.in/Comfed)), so bonus should be a manual, later feature.

## Farmer slips, bills and deductions are where Grocery Khata's existing strengths land

The farmer-facing side maps onto what Grocery Khata already has. Each entry should send a WhatsApp slip (litres, fat, SNF, rate, amount, running total), followed by a bill at the cycle close. The Amul model pays societies every 10 days ([Amul](https://Amul.com/files/pdf/6-10-15-Cow-to-consumer.pdf)) and Hamari Dairy offers 10-day, 15-day and monthly bills ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.hamari_dairy)), so the cycle must be a setting. Bihar's federation passbook shows quantity, quality, value, **cattle feed and cash payment** entries ([Patna Press](https://patnapress.com/?p=56553)), which confirms the deduction lines: the bill should be gross milk value minus cattle feed and mineral mixture sold on credit, advance recovery and other dues, giving net payable. Cattle feed sold to farmers is simply a sale from the centre's existing stock module charged to the farmer's khata, and advances are entries in the same khata, so the farmer is one party with both a payable (milk) and receivables (feed, advance) netted on one statement.

Message cost is manageable. A WhatsApp utility template costs about Rs 0.145, and from 1 October 2026 utility templates inside the 24-hour window are no longer free, according to a reseller that should be checked against Meta ([AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-pricing-update-october-2026/)); other guides give Rs 0.11 to 0.16 ([MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026)). At 100 farmers and two shifts that is about 6,000 messages, roughly **Rs 870 a month**, comparable to or cheaper than SMS, which competitors such as Meri Dairy charge for per entry ([SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/meri-dairy)). The shop should choose between per-shift slips, one daily summary, or bill-only messages, with free click-to-chat links from the owner's own WhatsApp as the zero-cost fallback. Paying farmers by UPI or bank from the bill closes the loop, and for Bihar cooperative members paid directly by DBT ([DairyNews Today](https://dairynews.today/news/bihar_s_dairy_cooperatives_perform_strongly_on_payments_and_development_5418849.html)) the bill becomes a statement of value and deductions rather than a cash payout. Cheap competition sets the price ceiling: small apps run from free to about Rs 2,000 a year, against roughly Rs 1 lakh a year for a full AMCS ([Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/liter-milk-collection-software); [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/prompt-automatic-milk-collection)).

## Dispatch reconciliation is the feature nobody else sells

The selling side treats Sudha or the private dairy as a party in the existing supplier-khata style, except that it is a buyer. Each dispatch (date, shift, can or tanker, litres or kg, average fat and SNF from the centre's own readings) is matched against the dairy's acceptance after its re-test, and the app shows the gap in kg, fat kg, SNF kg and rupees. That gap is where money disappears: documented frauds include fat cut by 0.1 to 0.5% and dummy farmer accounts ([Bharathi Dairy Farm](https://www.bharathidairyfarm.com/common-malpractices-by-milk-collection-agencies.php)), salt and sugar added to pass SNF ([Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/telangana-vijaya-dairy-milk-in-some-centres-found-to-be-adulterated)), faulty lactometers ([Deccan Chronicle](https://deccanchronicle.com/141117/nation-crime/article/milk-adulteration-rampant-karimnagar-district)), and tanker milk diverted through a hidden water compartment ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/involvement-of-insiders-suspected-in-milk-adulteration-scam-994897.html)). An immutable entry log with edit history, flags for unusual fat jumps per farmer, and per-dispatch loss trends protect the owner against both staff and buyer.

The centre's income also needs its own line. Unions pay societies a margin on top of chart value: about Rs 1 to 2 per litre in Karnataka ([dairynews7x7](https://dairynews7x7.com/news/consumers-pay-more-but-dairy-farmers-get-lesser-in-karnataka)), Rs 2 per litre for staff and office in Ernakulam ([dairynews7x7](https://dairynews7x7.com/news/ercmpu-to-pay-%e2%82%b97-extra-to-societies-farmers-to-get-%e2%82%b95-more-a-litre)), while Aavin deducts Rs 1.75 per litre from farmers for society costs ([National Herald](https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/national/tiruchy-dairy-farmers-flag-deductions-from-revised-milk-payments)). No COMFED commission figure was found. Under DBT a Bihar society's income is that commission, not the spread between collection and payout; for a private centre it is the spread. The app should therefore record commission as a separate receivable, reconcile the dairy's 10-day statement (litres, fat, SNF, amount, commission) line by line, and report the centre's profit after expenses from the existing expense book. Tax treatment should stay light: raw milk is nil-rated under GST ([taxguru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/gst-raw-milk-exemption-dispute-asmt-10-drc-07.html)), commission may attract 2% TDS under 194H above Rs 15,000 or 20,000 ([TaxBuddy](https://www.taxbuddy.com/blog/is-gst-applicable-on-commission-under-194h)), though whether unions deduct it from society commission is unconfirmed and needs a CA. FSSAI requires basic registration up to 500 litres a day and a state licence from 501 to 50,000 ([taxguru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/fssai-registration-process-dairy-units-including-milk-chilling-units.html)), which belongs in the shop profile as a reminder, not a workflow.

## Private centres are the first market; device sync is the later bridge

The market split decides what to build first. In Bihar, cooperative societies buy only about a third of marketed milk and private dairies and middlemen handle most of the rest ([ICAR study](https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IJAnS/article/view/24317)). Cooperative societies increasingly run NDDB AMCS, already in over 26,000 societies across 12 states ([PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/jan/doc202619754001.pdf)), or vendor DPMCUs that upload to the vendor's cloud over 4G ([Prompt DPMCU](https://www.promptdairytech.com/dpmcu-milk-collection-system/)), and Bihar is rolling these out with a farmer passbook portal ([Govt of Bihar](https://betastate.bihar.gov.in/Comfed)). Competing with that stack head-on is pointless, and no public export format was found for Prompt, Akashganga or Everest units. Private centres and village middlemen, by contrast, often have only a scale and a fat machine and use paper or a basic app, so **manual, offline-first entry in Hindi is the right first target**; offline entry with sync and Bluetooth printing are table stakes, as Hamari Dairy and MCS show ([AppGoblin MCS](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.milkcollectionsystem)).

| Phase | Build | Why now |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Farmer register (species, village, bank/UPI); shift entry with fat, SNF/CLR, litres or kg; rate plans (grid with Excel import, two-axis, fat-only, flat) versioned by date; WhatsApp slip; configurable 10-day/15-day/monthly bill with feed and advance deductions; dispatch log with dairy acceptance and loss report; Hindi/English; offline | Matches what centres do daily and adds the reconciliation competitors lack |
| 2 | Dairy statement import and line-by-line matching; commission receivable; fraud flags and edit audit; Bluetooth thermal printer; OCR of analyser or DPMCU slips; farmer self-view link | Deepens trust and speeds the queue without hardware partnerships |
| 3 | Direct Bluetooth/serial reading from analysers and scales; bonus and seasonal incentives; "sell to homes" doodhwala mode; TDS and FSSAI helpers | Higher effort or uncertain demand |

The doodhwala mode deserves its later slot rather than a separate shop type: MCS already combines milk purchase and sale with profit and loss ([AppGoblin MCS](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.milkcollectionsystem)), OkCredit targets household delivery khata ([OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/khata-app-for-dairy-business)), and many centres sell some milk locally, so one dairy shop type with "buy from farmers" and "sell to homes" modes fits better than two sign-ups.

## Conclusion

The defensible product is not a better collection screen but a **closed ledger with three parties**: farmers who want proof of every reading, a buyer whose re-test decides what the centre is paid, and an owner squeezed between them on a margin of a rupee or two per litre. Grocery Khata's khata, WhatsApp, stock and expense modules already cover two-thirds of that ledger; the new work is the rate engine and the buy-versus-accept reconciliation, and a fat error of a few tenths of a percent across hundreds of litres a day can outweigh the whole margin, which is why reconciliation sells.

Before writing product rules, the team should collect a real Sudha union rate chart, a dairy's 10-day statement and a few farmer slips from Bihar centres, because the research could not confirm COMFED's pricing basis, commission, payment cycle, cow/buffalo split or how shortages are settled. Those few paper documents will settle more design questions than further desk research.

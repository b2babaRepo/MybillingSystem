# Extend Grocery Khata with templates and notebooks

Grocery Khata can serve cement/building-material shops, paint shops and other trades without becoming a separate product per trade. It needs one core item model with a **business-type template chosen at onboarding**. The template switches on the right attribute fields, units, GST/HSN defaults and a seeded common catalogue. A few real behaviour modules sit on top: unit conversion with decimal quantities, delivery challans that convert to invoices, site-wise khata, composite "tinted" lines, and batch/serial tracking. This is how Vyapar and The Munim already handle items (generic fields plus optional secondary units), and BUSY and Marg sell trade-specific editions. Paint and cement dealers still report unmet needs, such as deducting a tinting base from stock and handling brass/cft sand units. Expenses should work like the *rokad bahi*: one page per day, each line with particulars, amount and mode, a running balance, and an evening cash count. Entry should be a single free-text or voice line such as "chai 40, auto 120, thaili 250 upi", parsed by the Claude + Sarvam pipeline the app already has. The big SME apps use forms, and the AI trackers that accept Hinglish voice are consumer apps with no link to a shop's cash book. That gap is Grocery Khata's opening. The notes come from short research runs that leaned on vendor pages, and no dealer has been interviewed, so field pilots should confirm the priorities below before any build.

## One item model plus trade templates beats separate editions

Indian billing apps do not build true per-category modules. Vyapar offers "Additional Item Fields" checkboxes (serial, batch, model, size), batch data with MRP and mfg/expiry, and a base unit with an optional secondary unit and conversion rate ([Vyapar guide](https://vyaparapp.in/guides/how-to-add-item-batches-serial-numbers-in-vyapar); [Vyapar units](https://vyaparapp.in/videos/how-to-add-inventory-units)). The Munim uses the same pattern ([The Munim](https://themunim.com/helpdesk/how-to-use-secondary-unit-option-for-item/)). Zoho Inventory generates variants from attributes and supports composite items with their own stock ([Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/inventory/help/items/item-groups.html)). These models have known limits. In Vyapar an item tracks **batch or serial, not both**, and Zoho users complain that lines for the same item merge even when units or custom fields differ ([Zoho community](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/community/topic/line-items-of-the-same-item-id-should-not-be-automatically-consolidated-as-it-fails-to-recognize-variation-in-custom-fields-as-well-as-distinct-items-of-the-same-id-sold-in-varying-units-of-measure?page=7)).

For Grocery Khata, keep the existing common catalogue with per-shop prices and add a `BusinessType` on the shop. Each template defines an attribute schema, stored as a JSON attribute bag on the item, validated against the template's definition in .NET. It also defines default units, GST/HSN defaults and a seeded catalogue. The catalogue becomes one per category, and per-shop price rows stay as they are. Never merge bill lines that differ in attributes or unit, and allow batch and serial on the same item.

| Template | Attributes switched on | Units / quantities | Special module |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kirana (current) | brand, pack | pcs, kg, g, L | none |
| Cement / building material | brand, grade (OPC/PPC), diameter (TMT), HSN | bag, kg, tonne, brass, cft, rod; decimals | challan→invoice, site khata, charge lines, credit limit/aging |
| Paint | brand, product, base, shade code, finish, pack | 1L/4L/10L/20L as **separate SKUs** | composite tinted line, painter tag |
| Hardware / electrical | model, serial, warranty | pcs, ft, m | serial tracking |
| Medical / FMCG with expiry | batch, mfg/exp | strips, pcs | batch FEFO, block expired stock |
| Clothing | size, colour | pcs | variant generation |

The template list beyond cement and paint follows the attribute patterns in the apps above. The notes did not research those trades directly.

## Cement shops bill in bags, brass and tonnes across three GST rates

A building-material bill mixes tax slabs. Since **22 Sep 2025 cement is at 18% GST, down from 28%** (Notification 09/2025) ([Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/cement-gst-percentage-rate-hsn-code-applicability/)). Ind-Ra estimated the cut lowers prices by about **₹30–35 per bag** ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/news/gst-rate-cut-to-lower-cement-prices-by-30-to-35-per-bag-reports-ind-ra-125091001256_1.html)). Sand and aggregates are at **5%**, clay and fly-ash bricks at **12%**, and TMT/structural steel, ceramic tiles and PVC pipes at **18%** ([Tally HSN guide](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/hsn-code-of-cement-bricks-sand/)). One site claims TMT fell to 5%, but Tally and Accountune say 18% ([Accountune](https://accountune.com/hsn-code-steel)). Some pages still show cement at 28%, so seeded GST data needs an "as of" date and an admin update path. Each item therefore needs HSN and GST rate, and each invoice line needs its own GST breakup.

Units are the second break from kirana. Sand is quoted per brass, and **1 brass = 100 cft ≈ 2.83 m³** ([InfraLens](https://infralens.in/prices/aggregates)). 2026 prices run about ₹55–85/cft for river sand, plus ₹15–40/cft transport ([ConstructionEstimatorIndia](https://constructionestimatorindia.com/?p=11306)). Prices swing hard: during a 2025 shortage, Nagpur sand went from ₹40 to **₹105–120/cft** ([Hitavada](https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2025/10/6/sand-mafia-creates-artificial-shortage-pushes-up-prices-to-rs-105-120cft.html)). That makes a **"today's rate" bulk-edit screen with rate history** a must-have, along with base/alternate units (bag = 50 kg, tonne = 1000 kg, brass = 100 cft), fractional quantities, and freight, loading and hamali as separate charge lines. The notes do not settle how GST applies to those charges, so ask a CA before fixing the tax logic. Goods over **₹50,000** need an e-way bill, valid 1 day per 200 km ([IRIS GST](https://irisgst.com/?p=52867)). Truckloads cross that line routinely, so bills need vehicle and transporter fields and a warning above the threshold. Portal integration can wait until after the MVP.

The customer side is different too. Competitors sell **site-wise invoices and inventory**, **delivery challans that convert to invoices** ([Vyapar](https://vyaparapp.in/free/invoicing-software/construction)), contractor ledgers across several sites ([myBillBook](https://mybillbook.in/free/invoicing-software/construction/)), challans that reduce stock on dispatch ([LEDGERS](https://ledgers.cloud/in/deliverychallan)), and contractor accounts with aging reports ([Relaso](https://billingpro.relaso.com/industry/hardware)). In Grocery Khata's terms, add an optional Site/Project under each khata customer and show balances per site and in total. Add a challan document that reduces stock, allows partial delivery, and combines into one invoice. Give each customer a credit limit and credit period, warn when a bill would go over the limit, show 0–30/31–60/61–90/90+ aging, and send WhatsApp reminders by due date. Payments need advances held as credit, cheques with status (pending, cleared, bounced) and post-dated dates, and allocation against specific bills. None of the competitor pages showed brass/cft handling or TMT length-to-weight conversion, so these are places to stand out. The 30–60 day credit period is unverified.

## Paint shops need a composite "tinted line" that no mainstream app handles

Paint dealers sell ready-mix SKUs and also shades tinted on demand from a base plus colourants. A paint retailer on myBillBook's feature board asked, with no official response, that billing shade 0765 Apex Shyne 20L should **deduct the base actually used from stock and add the ₹65 colourant cost** ([myBillBook featurebase](https://mybillbook.featurebase.app/p/need-to-changes-as-per-paint-retail-and-wholesale)). BUSY says it does base deduction, stored tinting recipes, partial-container sales, batches, schemes and painter commissions, but these are untested vendor claims ([BUSY](https://busy.in/accounting-software/paint-shop/)). Tinting-machine vendors such as Fast & Fluid sell separate dispensing software ([PrismaPro2](https://asia.fast-fluid.com/products/software/prismapro-2-pos-tinting-software)). Painter loyalty mostly runs through brand QR-scan schemes such as Berger "Lift and Win" and Birla Opus, outside the shop's billing ([Berger](https://bergerpaints.com/become-a-painter); [Anyline](https://anyline.com/?p=26765)). Paint (HSN 3208/3209/3210) also moved from 28% to **18%** on 22 Sep 2025 ([Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/paint-and-coatings-hsn-code-gst-rate/)).

Model this as a **composite bill line**: a base SKU (product × base × pack) that comes off stock, a shade code stored as a label rather than a stock item, and a tint charge, so price = base price + tint charge. Save shade-to-base mappings per shop so a repeat order is one tap. Treat each pack size as its own SKU, since each has its own MRP. A simple "painter/contractor" tag on the bill, with a commission report per painter, covers shop-level loyalty without copying brand schemes. Reading machine output and settling brand schemes are post-MVP, because the notes found no documentation on either.

## A one-line, voice-first rokad bahi makes expense entry as easy as paper

The shop notebook already has a format. The rokad bahi records **date, particulars, in, out, mode and balance**. Each day opens with yesterday's closing cash, and the evening box count must match: closing = opening + cash in − cash out ([Accountune](https://accountune.com/cash-book-format)). Typical lines are tempo/loading, auto fare, chai-nashta, packaging, string and tape, repairs, supplier payments, staff advances and rent. The two big problems are cash taken for home and never written down, and UPI receipts that go to the bank rather than the drawer, which stops the book from matching the box ([Accountune](https://accountune.com/cash-book-format)). Current apps use forms. Vyapar asks for a category, an optional GST toggle with party/HSN, a payment mode and a receipt image, and splits Direct from Indirect expenses to give gross versus net profit ([Vyapar guide](https://vyaparapp.in/guides/how-to-add-and-manage-expenses-in-vyapar-app)). myBillBook uses an expense voucher ([myBillBook help](https://knowledge.mybillbook.in/articles/8511360-how-to-create-an-expense-entry-in-mybillbook)). Hinglish voice entry exists only in consumer trackers: AiXpense uses Sarvam for code-mixed speech in 22 languages, and Munshi takes WhatsApp voice notes ([AiXpense](https://peerlist.io/pratikjadhav/project/aixpense--ai-expense-tracker); [Munshi](https://peerlist.io/atybdot/project/munshi--whatsapp-expense-tracker)). Ezy Rokad shows there is demand for software laid out like a handwritten cashbook ([Techjockey](https://techjockey.com/detail/ezyrokad)).

Build an **"Aaj ka panna"** view on top of the existing cash book. It shows one page per date, swipes between days, uses handwritten-style rows with a mode chip, and keeps a running total and cash-in-hand figure in view. At the bottom sits one text box with a mic. The user types or speaks "chai 40, auto 120, thaili 250 upi". Claude splits the line on commas, "aur" and new lines, reads Hindi number words ("chalis", "dedh sau"), maps each keyword to a category (auto/tempo/bhada → Transport, bijli → Electricity, kiraya → Rent, pagaar/advance + name → Staff, ghar/khud → Personal), and sets the mode from "upi/phonepe/gpay", otherwise Cash. The parsed chips appear for **one-tap confirm** before saving, the same pattern already used for grocery lists. Photographing a wholesaler or electricity bill reads total, date and vendor, suggests "Maal kharid" and keeps the image as a receipt. GST input credit stays an off-by-default option.

Start with these categories: Maal kharid, Kiraya, Bijli, Staff/advance, Bhada, Chai-pani, Packaging, Repair, Bank, Ghar kharcha and Other. Add big icon chips for the shop's top six, each pre-filled with its last amount. Rent, salary and electricity get monthly reminders, not automatic posting. The only required field is amount. Deleting strikes the line through rather than removing it, which matches the paper habit and keeps an audit trail. Only cash-mode lines change the drawer balance. **Ghar kharcha is booked as drawings**: it lowers cash in hand but stays out of profit and loss. Maal kharid counts as cost of goods and can link to a supplier khata. A **day-close step** compares expected cash (opening + cash sales + cash khata collections − cash expenses − withdrawals) with the counted cash and posts the gap as a short/excess line. A profit card shows Sales − Maal kharid − Kharcha = Munafa, with personal spending shown separately. The notes found no usability data on multi-item free-text entry by low-literacy users and no accuracy figures for Sarvam on Hinglish numbers, so both need a pilot.

## Conclusion

The way to widen Grocery Khata is to handle the *behaviours* each trade needs, not to make the catalogue bigger. Three modules unlock most of the market: unit conversion with decimals, document flow (challan → invoice, with site khata and aging), and composite lines (tinting now, kits and assemblies later). Templates then only need to configure attributes and seed catalogues, so a new trade means adding data rather than writing code. The expense notebook follows the same logic. Shopkeepers already know how to keep a bahi, so the app's job is to remove the friction of writing it down. Grocery Khata already has Claude + Sarvam parsing tied to a real cash book and khata, which neither the form-based SME apps nor the consumer AI trackers combine. Pick a few cement and paint dealers and run one voice-expense pilot to test this before building. Two things carry the most risk: GST on transport and loading charges, and how accurately the parser reads amounts. Settle both before any of this ships.

# Turn the stretched phone app into a desktop workspace

Grocery Khata on a laptop or desktop Chrome is still mostly the phone app made wider. A left sidebar and a green top bar wrap one content column that measured **about 1152px wide at 1366px**. Most screens fill that column with phone-style rows: the label sits at the far left, the value at the far right, and empty space lies between them. Only the bill screen has a real two-pane layout. The fix is not a second app. The fix is **one responsive codebase with real desktop layouts from about 1024px up**, which is what Material 3 guidance recommends: one pane on phones, list-detail with two panes on expanded widths, and a side rail in place of a bottom bar ([SAP Fiori, mirroring M3](https://www.sap.com/design-system/fiori-design-android/v26-4/foundations/adaptive-layout/canonical-layouts)). Within that codebase, about **ten of the seventeen screens reviewed need their own desktop page structure**. These are the customer ledger, orders, reports, P&L, cashbook, item picker, settings, staff, suppliers and dues. The rest need only a max-width and small tweaks. Billing is already dedicated but needs keyboard and density work. Three shared building blocks would cover most of the work: a desktop table, a list-detail layout and a fuller sidebar. Two screens could not be reviewed because the test shop (Neha Fashion, clothes.localhost) has no customers or orders: the customer ledger itself and the order detail. Their designs below are proposals, not fixes for problems anyone has seen.

Method: all screen findings come from a live, view-only review of the clothes shop as owner at 1366x768 and 1920x1080 on 2026-11-06, with page widths measured in the browser. Industry patterns are cited inline. The kirana shop was not reviewed.

## Three shell problems undermine every desktop screen

**The sidebar hides most of the app.** It lists only five items (Home, Khata/Udhaar, Bill, Orders, Items). Reports, Dues, P&L, GST, Cashbook, Expenses, Suppliers, Stocktake and Settings have no entry, so a desktop user always has to go back through Home. Below its five links the sidebar is mostly empty space. On wide screens (lg), add a "Money" group (Dues, Cashbook, Expenses, P&L, GST) and a "Shop" group (Reports, Stocktake, Suppliers, Settings). The phone bottom bar can stay as it is.

**Pages have no identity, and phone habits leak through.** Sub-pages show a mobile back arrow "‹" beside the shop name even when the sidebar is visible, and there is no page title or breadcrumb. On lg, show an H1 page title with a breadcrumb (Reports › Sizes), and put page actions (Add customer, Export) at the right of the header. Floating "+" buttons (add item, add customer) sit at the bottom right of a 1366–1920px screen, far from the content, so they belong in the header as normal buttons. The size/colour picker on the bill screen opens as a narrow bottom sheet even at 1920. On desktop it should be a centred dialog or a popover attached to the item tile.

**Width is wasted rather than used.** Short rows and forms run edge to edge across about 1150px. Primary buttons such as GST's "CA ki report (Tally export)" are about 1150px wide. One global rule fixes most of this: centre non-table pages at `max-w-3xl` (forms, settings), give reports `max-w-6xl`, and let tables run full width. Controls are touch-sized (44px buttons, 84px Home tiles, 16px Mukta/Noto Devanagari body text). That is fine for a phone but oversized for a mouse. A compact desktop density of 32–36px fits more rows on a 768px-tall laptop screen. SAP Fiori recommends compact table density on desktop and suggests letting users choose it ([SAP table usage](https://www.sap.com/design-system/btp/dna-design/floorplans/table/usage)). Finally, after a route change the old screen often stayed visible for up to about 2 seconds (for example, #/stocktake briefly showed the staff page). This looks like a bug to users. Show a skeleton straight away on navigation.

## Billing should copy Tally's keyboard, not Square's tiles

Billing matters most because a counter PC with a barcode scanner is where a desktop beats a phone. The industry splits into two families. Indian desktop software is keyboard-first. In TallyPrime, F8 opens Sales and F9 Purchase, Alt+G jumps to any report by name, and each key does the same thing on every screen ([Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/business-guides/tallyprime-keyboard-first-design-fast-data-entry/)). In Marg, F2 starts a new sale without leaving the billing screen ([Marg Care](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/billing/44648/1/How-to-create-New-Sale)). Global POS tools such as Shopify and Square use touch tile grids ([Style Factory](https://www.stylefactoryproductions.com/blog/shopify-pos-vs-square-pos)). Zoho POS ships both, as separate Standard (keyboard) and Touch modes, and a scanned barcode goes straight into the cart ([Zoho POS](https://www.zoho.com/en-us/pos/features/sales-management.html)). myBillBook's web POS adds "Hold & Create Another", which parks a bill and starts a new one, and it marks zero-stock items in red ([myBillBook KB](https://knowledge.mybillbook.in/articles/8679710-how-to-use-pos-billing-in-mybillbook-web-app)).

Grocery Khata's tiles suit loose, unbarcoded kirana items. But a clothes counter with barcode price tags fits the keyboard family. So keep the tiles and lead with the keyboard. A scanner types like a keyboard, so focus must return to the scan field after every item is added. The tile-first layout does not show this today, and keyboard and scanner input was not tested. Suggested keys: "/" or F2 to search, Enter to add, F4 for payment, Ctrl+S or Ctrl+Enter to save, Esc to clear, and a hold-bill key. Add a "?" cheat-sheet, as Zoho Billing does with Shift+/ ([Zoho Billing](https://www.zoho.com/billing/help/keyboard-shortcuts.html)). These keys are proposals and should be checked so they do not clash with Chrome's own shortcuts.

## Responsive with desktop layouts beats a separate desktop app

Separate desktop pages would be better only in the narrow sense that the screens above need their own layouts. A separate desktop app or route tree would double the upkeep for a small team. One user review also calls myBillBook's separate desktop app a buggy "beta" next to its web app ([Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/retail/flobooks-profile/reviews/)). The strongest case for a truly separate mode is billing, where Zoho's keyboard/touch split shows that both input styles are worth designing for on purpose. The practical answer is to choose a layout by breakpoint inside one app: at lg and up, swap in `<ListDetail>`, `<DesktopTable>` or the POS layout, and keep the phone layout below that.

## Conclusion

The problem is not that Grocery Khata lacks a desktop version. It already renders one: a sidebar around a phone column. The work is to let desktop screens be shaped by their data instead of by the phone's single column. Most of the gain comes from a small shared kit (sidebar groups, a table, list-detail) plus one carefully built POS screen. A keyboard-first bill page is also a real point of difference against touch-first rivals, because Indian shopkeepers who learned Tally already expect F-keys.

Open questions remain. The ledger and order detail designs need checking against real data. Keyboard and scanner flow was not tested. Most screens were measured at 1366 and assumed to stretch the same way at 1920. The kirana shop's layout may differ from the clothes shop's.

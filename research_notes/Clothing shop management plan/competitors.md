# Competitors: billing and inventory software for Indian clothing (garment) shops, 2026

Caveat: most sources are vendor marketing pages or vendor-authored comparisons. Few independent reviews of garment-specific features exist. Research was limited to roughly 12 tool calls.

## Which products serve garment shops, and how are they positioned?

### Takeaway
There are three tiers. First, mobile-first SMB apps: Vyapar, myBillBook, GimBooks, Accountune and Zakya. Second, desktop accounting suites with variant support: Busy, Marg and Tally plus attributes. Third, chain-grade apparel ERPs sold on dealer quotes: Ginesys, GoFrugal and LOGIC ERP. Garment-specific depth goes up through those tiers, and so do price and setup effort.

### Cited Findings
- myBillBook markets tracking by size (S to XXL), colour, style and brand, plus barcode scanner and thermal printer support, size exchanges and returns, and sales by brand and category, including seasonal reports — [myBillBook garment page](https://mybillbook.in/s/garment-shop-billing-software/)
- myBillBook pricing: Plus ₹3,490/yr (1 user), Pro ₹3,990/yr (adds the desktop app, label printing, unlimited godowns and WhatsApp/SMS marketing), Pro Max from ₹570/month (2 businesses, 3 users, e-invoice, loyalty and rewards, POS, Tally export on request). Loyalty is only in Pro Max — [myBillBook](https://mybillbook.in/s/garment-shop-billing-software/)
- A public myBillBook feature request asks for "Product Variants (Size & Color)" while creating items. This suggests variant creation is not native or streamlined. The request's date and status are unverified — [myBillBook Featurebase](https://mybillbook.featurebase.app/p/feature-request-add-product-variants-size-and-color-while-creating)
- Vyapar's garment pages say each size and colour combination is tracked as its own stock entry, with low-stock alerts by size, colour or design and a size/colour matrix on its POS page — [Vyapar clothing POS](https://vyaparapp.in/pos-software/clothing); [Vyapar cloth shop](https://api1.vyaparapp.in/free/billing-software-for-retail-shop/cloth-shop)
- Vyapar works offline on Windows PC and Android — [Capterra Vyapar reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/180579/Vyapar/reviews/)
- Busy (boutique page) tracks size, colour, style, shade, design number or SKU. It offers barcode label printing for unbarcoded garments, discounts and seasonal, clearance and festive schemes, and credit notes for returns and exchanges. Reports cover size- and colour-wise stock, fast and slow-moving styles, season-wise sales, discount and margin, and stock ageing. It controls price overrides and discounts through user permissions but has no salesman module listed. Plans are Start, Smart, Power and Power+ (no prices on the page), with a 15-day trial — [Busy boutique](https://busy.in/accounting-software/retail-shop/boutique/)
- Marg ERP 9+ enables size-wise stock by renaming the "Batch" field to "Size" in Control Room. This is a workaround, not a true matrix — [Marg Care](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/size/45865/1/How-to-enable-Size-wise)
- Marg is described as having a dated UI, server-based installs, dealer-quoted pricing and no self-serve trial. It is strong on size, shade and style stock alongside wholesale. (This is a competitor's opinion.) — [Accountune buyer's guide 2026](https://www.accountune.com/best-billing-software-for-garment-shop)
- TallyPrime handles variants through attributes, which makes setup heavy. It has no native mobile app, cloud access costs extra, and it has a high licence fee plus renewal. (This is Accountune's view.) — [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/best-billing-software-for-garment-shop)
- Accountune itself has a free plan and paid plans from ₹799/yr. It has a native size/colour/style matrix and automatically switches GST between 5% and 18% per piece by price (the ₹2,500 threshold it cites should be verified against the post-2025 GST rate changes). Its own page says it is cloud-only with no offline mode, no loyalty and no marketplace sync — [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/best-billing-software-for-garment-shop); [Accountune garment](https://accountune.com/garment-store-billing-software)
- Accountune says Vyapar is the only tool it compared that bills fully offline. It also says Vyapar's entry tier runs only on Android and Windows, and that its multi-outlet, wholesale and season/dead-stock reports are limited. It says GimBooks bills per metre but has thin reporting — [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/best-billing-software-for-garment-shop)
- GimBooks has a cloth-shop billing page — [GimBooks](https://www.gimbooks.com/billing-software-for-cloth-shop)
- GoFrugal is a multi-vertical platform that includes apparel: variants, barcode, replenishment and multi-location. RetailEasy edition prices are not public and buyers are sent to presales — [Capterra GoFrugal](https://www.capterra.com/p/71693/GoFrugal-POS-Software/); [Fitgap RetailEasy](https://us.fitgap.com/products/006832/gofrugal-retaileasy)
- Accountune says GoFrugal is partner-sold, aimed at chains and overbuilt for one or two outlets — [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/best-billing-software-for-garment-shop)
- Ginesys is described as a fashion-first ERP with native style-size-colour variants, seasonal and collection workflows, loyalty in its POS, and InsightX sell-through and margin analytics by style, size, colour, store and channel. It comes in desktop and cloud POS editions with subscription pricing (amounts not published). The source is a vendor-authored comparison — [Ginesys blog](https://ginesys.in/blog/ginesys-vs-gofrugal-fashion-retail-erp)
- LOGIC ERP offers a size, colour and design matrix, inter-store promotions and transfers, and loyalty. Accountune calls it overbuilt and overpriced for shops with fewer than five outlets — [LOGIC ERP blog](https://www.logicerp.com/blog/top-apparel-retail-store-brands-streamlining-garment-billing-with-logic-erp-garment-software/); [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/best-billing-software-for-garment-shop)
- Zoho's India retail POS is Zakya, launched in February 2024. It starts at ₹649/month billed annually and has a free tier — [Republic World 2024](https://www.republicworld.com/business/startups/zoho-launches-zakya-to-provide-pos-solution-for-retail-stores/?amp=1); [Capterra Zakya](https://www.capterra.in/software/1063821/zakya)
- RetailGraph's Capterra listing shows only generic features: barcode, purchase orders, returns and real-time reports. Nothing garment-specific was found — [Capterra RetailGraph](https://www.capterra.com/p/136947/RetailGraph/)
- GSoft ERP (formerly Alpha eBarcode's Gretail) claims style-colour-size variants. Its current page shows no alteration, commission or consignment features, although an older search snippet mentioned "multiple type of salesman commission" and alteration management — [GSoft ERP](https://www.gsofterp.com/)
- Smaller garment-specific players include BharatERP (colour- and size-wise modules), TheMunim and Relaso (size x colour grid with a barcode per cell) — [BharatERP](https://www.bharaterp.com/software/readymade-garment-shop-billing-software.html); [TheMunim](https://themunim.com/retail/clothing-store); [Relaso](https://billingpro.relaso.com/blog/matrix-inventory-sizes-colors-variants-fashion-retail)

### Inferences
- SMB apps such as myBillBook and Vyapar claim variant tracking, but their schemes, commission, alteration, ratio packs and consignment features are absent or unadvertised. That gap is open for a low-cost, mobile-first garment product.
- Desktop suites like Busy cover the reports a garment shop needs (season, ageing, margin) but ship as desktop software with tiered licences.
- Real apparel depth (seasons, sell-through by size, loyalty) sits in Ginesys and LOGIC ERP, which use opaque, dealer-led pricing.

### Gaps
- No primary sources were found for Swipe or Dukaan garment features. Dukaan appears mainly as an online storefront, with only 9 live Dukaan stores in men's clothing as of December 2025 — [StoreLeads](https://storeleads.app/reports/dukaan/category/Apparel/Men%27s%20Clothing)
- No public prices were found for Busy, Marg, GoFrugal, Ginesys, LOGIC ERP or Tally garment add-ons.

## Garment-specific features: size-ratio purchase, consignment, alteration, commission, schemes and loyalty

### Takeaway
Size/colour variants, barcode labels and returns via credit notes are table stakes. Buy-2-get-1 schemes, salesperson commission, alteration tickets, ratio-pack purchasing and sale-or-return consignment are rarely documented publicly for any Indian SMB product.

### Cited Findings
- LOGIC ERP's garment blog does not mention ratio packs, consignment, alteration, salesperson commission or loyalty — [LOGIC ERP blog](https://www.logicerp.com/blog/top-apparel-retail-store-brands-streamlining-garment-billing-with-logic-erp-garment-software/)
- Ginesys's comparison does not cover ratio purchasing, consignment or schemes — [Ginesys blog](https://ginesys.in/blog/ginesys-vs-gofrugal-fashion-retail-erp)
- myBillBook's garment page does not mention discount schemes, salesman tracking or offline use — [myBillBook](https://mybillbook.in/s/garment-shop-billing-software/)
- A size ratio describes order mix. For example, 100/200/200/100 units of S/M/L/XL gives a ratio of 1:2:2:1 — [Online Clothing Study](https://onlineclothingstudy.com/2018/05/what-is-size-ratio-in-fashion-industry.html)
- In Marg, "Consignment" is one of the renamable batch-type fields alongside Size, which implies a basic consignment field — [Marg Care](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/size/45865/1/How-to-enable-Size-wise)

### Inferences
- A product that documents and demos ratio-pack purchasing, sale-or-return consignment from brands, alteration tickets and salesman commission would stand out, because competitors either lack these features or bury them in dealer demos.

### Gaps
- Busy, Marg, GoFrugal and Ginesys documentation on commission, alteration and consignment was not reached in this pass.

## Reviewer complaints, offline support and mobile vs desktop

### Takeaway
Common complaints are data integrity, barcode printing, support quality and too many clicks at a busy counter. Offline billing is a strong differentiator that only Vyapar and the desktop suites offer. Cloud-first apps such as Accountune lack it.

### Cited Findings
- Vyapar is rated 4.3/5 on Capterra (181 reviews). Complaints include data that "disappeared" in year-end calculations, unresolved barcode printing problems, hangs, no offline support team, and too much manual clicking at the counter. A garment-store reviewer praises its GST bills on WhatsApp — [Capterra Vyapar](https://www.capterra.com/p/180579/Vyapar/reviews/); [Capterra IN](https://www.capterra.in/reviews/180579/vyapar); [Trustpilot](https://dk.trustpilot.com/review/vyaparapp.in)
- myBillBook runs on Android, iOS and web, and on desktop with Pro — [myBillBook](https://mybillbook.in/s/garment-shop-billing-software/)
- Ginesys is described as "suited for offline retail" (Ginesys's characterisation of GoFrugal) and has desktop and cloud editions — [Ginesys blog](https://ginesys.in/blog/ginesys-vs-gofrugal-fashion-retail-erp)

### Inferences
- A winning SMB garment product needs offline-first counter billing, reliable barcode label printing on cheap thermal printers, and minimal taps per bill.

### Gaps
- No Play Store review mining was done. Independent reviews of Ginesys, LOGIC ERP and Marg garment modules were not collected.

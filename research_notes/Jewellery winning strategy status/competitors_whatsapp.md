# Indian jewellery software competitors: pricing and WhatsApp practices (Nov 2026)

## Competitor plans, prices, limits and weaknesses

### Takeaway
None of the vendors publish full, clear pricing. Most figures come from listing sites like Techjockey, Capterra and IndiaMART, or from a comparison blog run by a competitor. The common pattern is desktop software at about ₹10k–26k one-time plus a yearly AMC/ARC, or cloud software at about ₹10k–35k per year. No product called "SwarnaVyapar" could be found; the closest names are SwarnApp and Vyapar.

- A cheap yearly cloud plan with a free tier and native WhatsApp would undercut desktop plus AMC models. Marg, Busy and Omunim all charge recurring fees on top of the licence.
- The ₹5,500–₹40,000/year range in the claim is roughly consistent with the paid prices found (about ₹3.4k–48k).

### Gaps
- **Free-tier cap claim not verified:** No source confirmed the claim that rivals cap free tiers at about 100 tags or 5 girvi loans. Treat it as unconfirmed.
- **SwarnaVyapar:** No such product was found.
- **Logic ERP and JewelSoft:** Pricing was not researched due to the tool budget.
- **Not confirmed for any vendor:** languages, weighing-scale support, gram-based schemes and loss-of-value alerts on girvi loans.
- **Vendor pricing pages:** None were fetched; all figures are from listing sites or blogs.

## Daily rate cards and how jewellers share rates

### Takeaway
No software was found that offers a one-tap branded rate-card image for WhatsApp Status. Existing tools stop at rate alerts, PDF quotes, branded customer apps or WhatsApp Channels.

### Cited Findings
- **SwarnaPro:** You update the gold rate once and it applies everywhere; PDF quotations can be shared via WhatsApp or email — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757679306)
- **Jeweller-facing rate apps:** Ravindra Jewellery's app gives live gold and silver rates and alerts; Sarafa Bazar India gives B2B live rates — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1438211430), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1539477470)
- **WhatsApp Channels:** Gold-rate channels already exist on WhatsApp — [WhatsApp channel](https://whatsapp.com/channel/0029VaZCAMFDZ4LVu7RBQF3e)
- **Brand apps:** Jewellers launch branded apps to answer repeated rate queries and promote them over WhatsApp — [Retail Jeweller India](https://retailjewellerindia.com/?p=49223)

### Inferences
- Jewellers commonly post rates by hand-made image to Status and broadcast lists. This is a plausible gap for a one-tap card, but it is not sourced.

### Gaps
- No source showed what a typical jeweller's rate-card image looks like.
- No usage figures were found on Status vs broadcast vs Business catalog.

## WhatsApp mechanics: wa.me, Business API costs, broadcast limits, Web Share

### Takeaway
Free options (wa.me links, 256-contact broadcast lists, Status shared from the web app) are manual. The Business API automates reminders at about ₹0.115 per utility message and ₹0.8631 per marketing message (India, from 1 Oct 2026), and every template needs approval.

### Cited Findings
- **Billing model:** Meta bills per delivered template message (since July 2025) — [Meta](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- **Rates from 1 Oct 2026 (secondary reporting):** marketing ₹0.8631, utility and service ₹0.115, with the first 1,000 service messages per number each month free — [TelecomTalk](https://telecomtalk.info/whatsappbusiness-price-reveal-telegram-arattai-may-benefit/1012403/)
- **Conflicting rates:** Older or alternative figures are utility ₹0.145 and marketing ₹1.09 — [AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-business-pricing/)
- **Broadcast lists:** Up to 256 contacts per list, and only contacts who have saved your number receive them — [WhatsApp FAQ](https://whatsapp.com/faq/iphone/23782313)
- **Broadcast monthly cap (unverified):** A 35/month cap in some regions is reported but unverified — [Qiscus](https://www.qiscus.com/en/blog/whatsapp-broadcast-limit/)
- **Web Share API:** `navigator.canShare({files})` and `navigator.share` work with File objects where supported; HTTPS only and gated by the `web-share` permissions policy — [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/canShare)

### Inferences
- **wa.me links:** They open a chat with prefilled text only and cannot attach images. Each send needs a manual tap, so they suit one-to-one reminders and are free. (This is general knowledge, not from a fetched source.)
- **Web Share on Android:** In Chrome on Android, Web Share with a PNG file brings up the share sheet. The user can pick WhatsApp and then choose Status, which makes a one-tap rate card feasible from a PWA. `share()` must be called from a user gesture.
- **Per-shop API cost:** 500 utility reminders a month would cost about ₹58 at ₹0.115 each.

### Gaps
- Meta's official India rate card page was not fetched, so the rates above are from secondary sources.
- The exact template approval turnaround was not sourced.
- The MDN browser compatibility table did not load, so Chrome Android file-share support is from general knowledge.

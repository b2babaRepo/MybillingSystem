# Reports for small Indian shops: what apps offer, how they show them, what comes first (researched Nov 2026)

## Which reports do the leading apps offer?

### Takeaway
The apps share a common core: sales and purchase lists, daybook, cash flow or cash in hand, profit and loss (overall and per bill or item), stock summary and low stock, and customer statements with ageing of what each customer owes. Paid tiers add GST reports (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B), trial balance and balance sheet. Ledger-first apps (Khatabook Cashbook, OkCredit) centre on cash in and out and customer balances rather than profit.

### Cited Findings
- **Vyapar transaction reports:** Sale, Purchase, Daybook (filter by date, firm, godown), All Transactions, Profit and Loss (uses opening and closing stock), Bill Wise Profit, Cash Flow (running balance), Sale Aging (amount pending per customer and days overdue), Trial Balance, Balance Sheet. Most export to Excel or print. Only Sale and Purchase have a graph view. — [Vyapar guide](https://vyaparapp.in/guides/how-to-check-transaction-reports-in-vyapar-app)
- **Vyapar profit and stock reports:** Item-wise P&L and Party-wise P&L. Stock Summary shows quantity and value per item. Low Stock Summary lists items below their minimum. — [Vyapar P&L](https://vyaparapp.in/use-cases/profit-and-loss-reports); [Vyapar features](https://vyaparapp.in/blog/vyapar-app-features/)
- **myBillBook:** Party Statement (Ledger), Daybook, P&L, Stock Summary, and GSTR-1, GSTR-2 and GSTR-3B. GSTR-1 downloads as JSON for the GST portal, and GSTR reports can be auto-emailed to the CA. Vendor pages disagree on whether GSTR reports are free or paid. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1612498254); [help: party statements](https://knowledge.mybillbook.in/articles/1989927-how-to-view-and-share-party-statements-on-mybillbook); [CA page](https://mybillbook.in/ca-icai)
- **Swipe:** A daily overview of sales, pending versus paid invoices, and bank money in and out. Top-selling products, products near expiry, fast and slow-moving stock, repeat and top customers. Also a P&L, GSTR-1 and "40+ reports". Advanced analytics is premium, from Rs 2,799 a year. — [Swipe Insights](https://getswipe.in/blog/insights)
- **Khatabook Cashbook app:** Works out cash in hand, net balance, running balance and online balance automatically. Daily summary of the day's transactions. Monthly, day-wise, cash-in, cash-out and custom-date reports. Bill photos can be attached to entries. Third-party listing. — [Apptopia](https://apptopia.com/google-play/app/com.khatabook.cashbook/about)
- **Khatabook main app:** Live stock status, stock history with sale and purchase price, and low-stock alerts. Unverified third-party summary. — [Krowdbase](https://www.krowdbase.com/software/khatabook)
- **Khatabook reminders:** Automated SMS or WhatsApp reminders to customers who owe money. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/sellers/khatabook)
- **PhonePe Business:** The home screen shows total amount, average amount and number of transactions for a period. The settlement report covers successful payments only and arrives the next day by 8 a.m., after commissions and refunds are taken off. Failed payments are in a separate transaction report. — [PhonePe help](https://cms.phonepe.com/en/mx/merchant-help/issues-customer-payments/what-if-i-havent-received-money-successful-customer-payment/daily-settlement); [settlement report](https://cms.phonepe.com/en/mx/merchant-help/reports-invoices/reports/how-can-i-view-or-download-my-settlement-report); [dashboard docs](https://developer.phonepe.com/docs/business-dashboard/home)
- **Paytm Business:** A day-level Ledger Report, a Settlement Report, and a Settlements Overview (amount, payments, deductions). Reports can be scheduled by email or SFTP. — [Paytm docs](https://www.paytmpayments.com/docs/settlement-reports-on-dashboard)
- **Zoho Books:** A GSTR-1 summary report, and customer statements as PDF or XLS. — [Zoho KB](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/kb/reports); [customer statement](https://www.zoho.com/billing/kb/reports/customer-statement-report.html)

### Inferences
- The minimum set across apps is: daybook, cash in hand, sales total, udhaar outstanding per customer, stock summary and low stock, and a simple P&L. GST reports, trial balance and balance sheet are for paid or GST-registered shops.
- For PhonePe and Paytm, the main report answers "how much reached my bank". Matching UPI totals inside a shop app would fill a real gap.

### Gaps
- No usable official 2024–26 report lists for OkCredit, Marg or Dukaan, and nothing on staff reports.
- No first-party Khatabook report list for 2026.

## How do apps present reports on a phone?

### Takeaway
Reports reach people in three ways: a daily summary pushed to the owner (Swipe sends it on WhatsApp and email); statements shared with customers as PDF on WhatsApp or SMS; and exports for the accountant (Excel, PDF, GSTR JSON, or auto-email to the CA). Charts are secondary: numbers and lists dominate, and only a few reports have a graph view.

### Cited Findings
- **Swipe daily summary:** Turned on under Settings > Auto reminders > Reminders to me ("Daily summary" for email and WhatsApp). — [Swipe community](https://community.getswipe.in/t/how-to-turn-on-daily-business-report/4395)
- **myBillBook statements:** Downloaded as PDF or shared as PDF on SMS or WhatsApp. GSTR reports can be auto-emailed to the CA, and accountants can get direct access. — [help](https://knowledge.mybillbook.in/articles/1989927-how-to-view-and-share-party-statements-on-mybillbook); [CA page](https://mybillbook.in/ca-icai)
- **Vyapar:** Excel export and print. Graphs only for Sale and Purchase. — [Vyapar guide](https://vyaparapp.in/guides/how-to-check-transaction-reports-in-vyapar-app)
- **Paytm:** A G2 reviewer said the daily settlement reports show earnings "without doing manual math". — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/paytm-business)
- **WhatsApp reach:** 97% of retail MSMEs surveyed use WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business (PayNearby MSME Digital Index 2024, via a press summary). — [Entrepreneur India](https://entrepreneur.com/en-in/news-and-trends/whatsapp-and-whatsapp-business-dominate-msme-landscape-with/476232)

### Inferences
- A WhatsApp end-of-day message is the natural channel for a simple app: sales, cash versus UPI, udhaar given and collected, and low stock.

### Gaps
- No sourced detail on voice or Hindi reporting, or on Tally export from Khatabook or OkCredit.

## What do customers get on their side?

### Takeaway
Customers mainly get a khata or party statement (a PDF of purchases, payments and balance) and payment reminders. No evidence of monthly household spend summaries in leading apps.

### Cited Findings
- myBillBook's statement covers opening balance, invoices and payments, and is shared on WhatsApp or SMS. — [myBillBook help](https://knowledge.mybillbook.in/articles/1989927-how-to-view-and-share-party-statements-on-mybillbook)
- Khatabook sends WhatsApp and SMS reminders for dues. — [G2](https://www.g2.com/sellers/khatabook)

### Inferences
- A monthly household spend summary could set the app apart, but it is unproven, so it should come later.

### Gaps
- No studies on what households find useful in kirana statements.

## What do shopkeepers actually look at?

### Takeaway
Hard data on which reports shopkeepers read is scarce. The studies measure digital adoption and WhatsApp use, not report use. Reviews praise simple "what did I earn today" views.

### Cited Findings
- 68% of retail MSME owners report growth after adopting digital tech (PayNearby 2024, more than 10,000 shops). — [IndiaRetailing](https://www.indiaretailing.com/2024/06/29/68-msme-retail-owners-witness-growth-in-business-post-adoption-of-digital-tech-report)
- Rs 74 of every Rs 100 of retail spend in top cities is paid digitally (NeoGrowth, 21,000 outlets), so UPI is a large share of daily takings. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/business/digital-payments-now-74-of-retail-spend-in-top-indian-cities-3744178)
- 54% of Indian shopkeepers plan to use more digital tools for sourcing and delivery (Flourish/Bain study, possibly 2022 data). — [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/smbstory/india-msme-kirana-owner-tapping-tech-digitisation)

### Inferences: suggested order for a simple app
1. Today's summary: sales, cash versus UPI, udhaar given and collected, cash in hand. Also sent on WhatsApp.
2. Udhaar outstanding per customer with ageing, a shareable PDF statement, and a reminder.
3. Low stock list, then stock value.
4. Item-wise sales and margin, fast and slow movers, and a monthly P&L.
5. Purchase versus sale, expenses, and Excel or PDF export for the accountant.
6. GST reports (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B) for paid or GST-registered shops only. Staff reports later.

### Gaps
- No survey ranking which reports kirana owners open most, and no Play Store review analysis.

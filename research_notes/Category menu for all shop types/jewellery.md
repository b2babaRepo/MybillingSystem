# Jewellery shop (gold, silver, diamond, imitation): category menu, filters, search, counter lookup

Method note: these notes come from web search results only. The retailer sites (tanishq.co.in, caratlane.com, bluestone.com, giva.co) were blocked by this session's network, so their live menus and filter panels could not be opened. Category and filter details come from search snippets of the retailers' own pages, which are primary but may be cached or stale. Sources marked **(secondary)** are blogs, resellers, review listings or calculators, not the retailer, vendor or regulator.

## 1. How the big Indian retailers organise categories

### Takeaway
Every major retailer's main axis is **product type** (rings, earrings, necklaces, chains, pendants, bangles, bracelets, mangalsutra, nose pin, coins). Below each type there is a **style** layer (for example jhumka, stud, hoop, bali for earrings), and **metal**, **gender**, **occasion** and **collection** appear as menu tabs or filters. Silver and fashion brands such as GIVA add **anklets** and **toe rings** as top-level types. Coins and bars are a separate department with weight-led browsing.

## 2. Filters used

### Takeaway
The common filters are price range, metal, purity, metal colour, gender, occasion/theme, gemstone/stone, style, size (ring/bangle) and stock or delivery status. A grams **weight-range filter** is confirmed for Candere and CaratLane (and for coins at BlueStone), but not for Malabar. HUID and making-charge type are **not** used as shopper filters on retail sites. They are attributes shown on product and invoice pages.

### Cited Findings
- **CaratLane:**
  - Purity filter values include 10 KT, 14 KT, 18 KT, 22 KT, Platinum 950 and Silver 925, with item counts beside each.
  - Metal + colour combinations include 14 KT Yellow / White / Rose Gold and 18 KT Rose Gold.
  - Other filters: price range, size, ring style, theme, gemstone, on sale and ready to ship.
  - Sources: [CaratLane US fashion rings 14KT](https://www.caratlane.us/jewelry/fashion+rings-14kt+gold.html), [18K rings](https://www.caratlane.us/jewelry/18kt+gold-rings.html), [bangles 14KT](https://www.caratlane.us/14kt+gold-18+kt+yellow+gold-bangles?jewellery_type=46&metal=43&purity=801) (US storefront).
  - The India site shows weight bands of 2–5 g, 5–10 g, up to over 30 g. Source: [CaratLane gold bangles](https://www.caratlane.com/jewellery/gold-bangles.html), from a search snippet (unverified live).
- **Candere:**
  - Filters: purity (18K, 22K with counts), metal colour, weight bands (1–2 g, 2–3 g, … 10–15 g), stock status, occasion, rating and chain type.
  - Coins and bars use narrow weight bands (0–1 g) at 24K.
  - Sources: [Candere 1 g gold bar](https://www.candere.com/gifts/gold-bar/1+gram.html), [Kalyan gold chains](https://www.candere.com/jewellery/kalyan-jewellers-gold-chain.html).
- **Malabar:** Brands, Product Type, Stock Status, Price, Product Size, plus "More Filters". No weight filter was seen. Sources: [Malabar gold jewellery](https://malabar191.malabargoldanddiamonds.com/gold-jewellery.html), [women](https://malabargoldanddiamonds.com/gold-jewellery/women.html).
- **BlueStone:** Price, Gender (Women, Men, Kids, Unisex), Occasion (for example Weekend, Akshaya Tritiya, Romance, Everyday). Coins have purity and weight filters. Sources: [BlueStone jewellery](https://www.bluestone.com/jewellery.html), [gold coins](https://bluestone.com/jewellery/goldcoins.html).
- **Tanishq:** price band, "Metal & Stones", gender (women, men, kids) and occasion/wear type. Product pages show karat and colour (for example "22K / Yellow") and gross and net weight. Sources: [Tanishq bestsellers](https://www.tanishq.com/jewelry/tanishq-bestsellers), [Classy Ring Mangalsutra product](https://www.tanishq.com/product/classy-ring-mangalsutra-up1919yevaa00.html).
- **Joyalukkas:** price band, shop-for (him / her / kids), style. Source: [Joyalukkas all jewelry](https://www.joyalukkas.com/us/jewelry/all-jewelry.html).
- **GIVA / BigBasket:** gender, jewellery type, material (925 Silver). Source: [BigBasket GIVA](https://www.bigbasket.com/pb/giva/jewellery/) (secondary).
- **BIS hallmarking and HUID**, which define the purity values to offer:
  - HUID is a 6-character alphanumeric code, mandatory for the sale of hallmarked gold jewellery since April 2023.
  - Mandatory gold grades are 14, 18, 20, 22, 23 and 24 karat. 9K (375) has a provision in the standard but is described as voluntary. Source: [News on AIR, Mar 2026](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/govt-adds-7-more-districts-in-6th-phase-of-mandatory-gold-hallmarking).
  - **Conflict:** several July 2025 reports say 9K hallmarking became mandatory. Sources: [newkerala](https://www.newkerala.com/news/o/bis-makes-hallmarking-karat-gold-mandatory-436), [Zee Biz](https://www.zeebiz.com/markets/commodities/news-hallmarking-of-9-carat-gold-made-mandatory-today-by-bureau-of-indian-standards-bis-hallmark-for-20-karat-22-karat-23-karat-24-karat-18-karat-14-karat-gold-373953) (secondary).
  - HUID can be verified in the BIS CARE app. Source: [Kuberbox guide](https://www.kuberbox.com/blog/verify-huid-code-step-by-step-bis-care-app/) (secondary).
- **Silver hallmarking:**
  - IS 2112:2025 sets a 3-part mark (BIS mark with "SILVER", fineness grade, HUID). Grades are 800, 835, 925, 958, 970, 990 and 999. HUID-based silver hallmarking started on 1 Sep 2025.
  - **Conflict:** some sources call silver HUID voluntary ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/government-introduces-voluntary-silver-jewellery-hallmarking-with-digital-traceability-3712528)). Others say HUID is compulsory for silver that is hallmarked ([Business Today, Dec 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/investment/story/silver-jewellery-gets-safer-govt-makes-huid-compulsory-for-hallmarked-silver-boosting-transparency-consumer-trust-505408-2025-12-06), [Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/viewcontent/social-welfare/social-awareness/consumer-education/hallmarking-of-jewellery/hallmarking-of-silver)). The likely reconciliation is that hallmarking silver is voluntary, but a hallmarked piece must carry HUID. This is unverified.
  - About 77 lakh silver pieces were HUID-hallmarked in the first year. Grades 925 and 800 make up about 95% of them. Source: [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/over-77-lakh-silver-jewellery-pieces-get-huid-hallmarking-since-september-2025-4127526).

### Inferences
- Suggested filter set for the app:
  - Metal (Gold / Silver / Platinum / Imitation)
  - Purity, driven by metal:
    - gold: 24K (999 / 995), 22K (916), 20K, 18K (750), 14K (585), 9K (375)
    - silver: 999, 925, 800
    - platinum: 950
  - Metal colour (yellow / white / rose)
  - Weight range in grams, with bands like Candere's: <1, 1–2, 2–3, 3–5, 5–10, 10–15, 15–30, 30+. Coin bands: 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 8, 10, 20, 50, 100 g.
  - Price range
  - Stone (plain / diamond / solitaire / gemstone / pearl / CZ / kundan-polki)
  - Gender (women / men / kids / unisex)
  - Occasion (daily / office / party / festive / wedding-bridal / gifting)
  - Size (ring size, bangle size such as 2.4 / 2.6 / 2.8)
  - In stock / at this branch
  - Hallmarked yes/no
- HUID and making-charge type are better as counter-only filters or search fields than as customer filters.

### Gaps
- No retailer was found exposing an HUID or making-charge filter to shoppers.
- Exact live price-band breakpoints for each retailer were not captured.
- Bangle size conventions (2.2 to 2.10 inch "size" notation) were not sourced in this pass.

## 3. Finding items at the counter in Indian jewellery software

### Takeaway
Counter software is **tag-centric**. Each piece gets a unique tag or barcode (often RFID or QR) printed with gross weight, net weight and purity, frequently straight from a connected scale. Lookup is by tag or barcode scan, item code or design number, HUID, weight, purity or karat, and image catalogue. Karigar (artisan) accounts are tracked by gross and fine weight.

### Cited Findings
- **Omunim** (vendor pages):
  - The software reads weight from the scale and auto-prints a barcode with gross weight. Barcode, QR and RFID tags are customisable and print on thermal or laser printers.
  - Items can be searched by HUID, the 6-character BIS ID.
  - It handles GST invoices, quotations, orders, approvals and estimates. Stock reports show carat, purity and item code.
  - Sources: [Omunim](https://omunim.com/), [Omunim billing](https://omunim.com/jewellery-billing-software), [Omunim software](https://www.omunim.com/jewellery-software.html).
  - Billing by weight, wastage and purity: [Techjockey listing](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/omunim-accounting-software) (secondary).
- **Marg ERP 9+ Jewellery:**
  - Stock can be held by weight, set or design. Karigar management covers receipts of finished goods and reconciling karigar accounts by gross weight, fine weight and diamond or stone quality.
  - Catalogue by image, carat, weight, item code and purity, with FIFO or average valuation.
  - Tags can be split at the point of sale to create a new tag.
  - Sources: [SoftwareSuggest Marg](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/marg-jewellery), [IndiaMart Marg](https://www.indiamart.com/proddetail/marg-erp-9-jewellery-software-19379334012.html), [Marg Multisolutions reseller](https://margmultisolutions.com/retail-software/jewellery-software/) (secondary/reseller).
- **GoFrugal (Sapphire):** listed with "GST split, karat-wise, HUID" strengths. Source: [codingclave 2026 comparison](https://codingclave.com/guides/best-jewelry-shop-software-india-2026) (secondary).
- **Vyapar:** the scale feeds weight into the bill, the day's gold rate is applied, and a GST bill prints. Source: [Vyapar jewellery POS](https://vyaparapp.in/pos-software/jewellery) (vendor).
- **Odoo jewellery POS module:** prices are calculated from gross, net and stone weight, and the gold rate is set in POS settings. Source: [Odoo scs_pos_gold_and_jewelry_billing](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/scs_pos_gold_and_jewelry_billing).
- Labels carry gross weight, net weight and purity. Buyer checklists cover item code creation, barcode scanning at billing and multi-counter billing. Sources: [myBillBook](https://mybillbook.in/s/jewellery-billing-software/), [GimBooks guide](https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/best-jewellery-billing-software/) (secondary).
- GoldMatrix and similar ERPs do metal accounting (gold, silver, platinum), karat and purity tracking, barcode and RFID scanning, gold rate management and SMS alerts. Sources: [G2 GoldMatrix](https://www.g2.com/sellers/gold-matrix), [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/jewellery-software-4/) (secondary).

### Inferences
- The counter search box should accept these inputs and auto-detect the type:
  - tag or barcode number (scan)
  - HUID (6 alphanumeric characters)
  - design or style number
  - item name in English, Hindi or regional language
- Two quick-filter inputs belong beside the search box: a weight lookup ("around 10 g ± tolerance") and purity.
- Secondary lookup fields: karigar or supplier, lot, counter or tray, branch.
- Each tagged piece is unique (quantity 1). A category tile at the counter should list tags in stock (with weight shown) rather than a generic SKU with quantity.

### Gaps
- No official manuals were retrieved for JewelSoft / Jewel Easy, GoFrugal jewellery or Logic ERP. Their exact search fields are unconfirmed.
- Whether HUID is validated against the BIS database inside these products is unconfirmed.

## 4. Live gold rate and per-item price (weight × rate + making)

### Takeaway
The standard formula is: metal value = net weight × day's rate for the karat. Making charge is added as ₹ per gram or as a % of metal value. Some shops also add wastage (VA) as a %. Stone or diamond value is added separately, and 3% GST is applied on the total. Software keeps a daily rate table per metal and purity that cashiers update, and labels or tags hold weights rather than fixed prices.

### Cited Findings
- Price formula: net weight × per-gram rate for the karat, plus making (% or ₹/g), plus wastage, plus GST. Karat rate can be derived as 24K rate × karat ÷ 24. Wastage is typically about 6–14%. Sources: [CaratLane blog](https://www.caratlane.com/blog/how-to-calculate-the-price-of-your-gold-jewellery/), [jwero making charges calculator](https://jwero.ai/tools/making-charges-calculator), [goldc.app](https://goldc.app/in/calculators/jewelry-price) (secondary).
- GST is a single 3% on the total (1.5% CGST + 1.5% SGST), including making charges. 5% applies to job work only. One calculator instead splits 3% on gold and 5% on making, which conflicts with the others. Sources: [devcalc](https://www.devcalc.in/gold-making-charges-calculator), [caakhilesh](https://caakhilesh.in/calculators/gold-making-charge/) (secondary).
- Worked example: ₹5,500/g × 10 g + 10% making = ₹60,500, + 3% GST ₹1,815 = ₹62,315. Source: [whitegold.money](https://whitegold.money/how-to-calculate-gold-jewelry-price) (secondary).
- Gold value is based on net metal weight. For example, a 15 g necklace with 3 g of stones is valued on 12 g. Source: [myBillBook blog](https://mybillbook.in/blog/choose-jewellery-billing-software-inventory-management/) (secondary).
- Tanishq product pages show gross and net weight separately. Source: [Tanishq product](https://www.tanishq.com/product/classy-ring-mangalsutra-up1919yevaa00.html).
- The current gold rate is set in POS settings ([Odoo module](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/19.0/scs_pos_gold_and_jewelry_billing)), and the day's rate is applied at billing ([Vyapar](https://vyaparapp.in/pos-software/jewellery)). A buyer guide advises checking that the rate used on a past bill can be reviewed ([myBillBook](https://mybillbook.in/blog/?p=10066), secondary). A reviewer of The Jewel Software found changing the gold rate and re-checking records confusing ([Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/retail/the-jewel-software-profile/), secondary).
- Imitation jewellery is HSN 7117, and gold and silver jewellery is HSN 7113. The GST rate for 7117 conflicts across sources:
  - 3%: [Busy](https://busy.in/hsn/sub-chapter-7117/), [GimBooks](https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/artificial-jewellery-hsn-code-gst-rates-7117-imitation-jewellery/)
  - 0%: [RegisterKaro](https://www.registerkaro.in/hsn/gst-rate-hsn-code-7117)
  - 12%: [Aditya Birla Capital](https://www.adityabirlacapital.com/abc-of-money/chapter-71-gst-rate-hsn-code-for-imitation-jewellery), likely outdated
  - All of these are secondary.

### Inferences
- The app needs a persistent "Today's rate" bar: 24K / 22K / 18K gold per gram (or per 10 g), silver per kg or per gram, and the time of last update. Cashiers set it each morning, and every bill stores a snapshot of the rate it used.
- Item tiles should show a computed price: net weight × rate[purity] + making (₹/g, %, or flat) + wastage % + stone value, then 3% GST. Price should recompute when the rate changes.
- Imitation items have a fixed MRP and are not rate-linked.
- Coins should be priced at the 24K rate plus a small premium.

### Gaps
- No live gold-rate feed or API source (for example IBJA rates) was researched.
- How retailers display the rate on site headers was not verified.
- The GST rate on imitation jewellery is unresolved and needs checking against the current notification.

## 5. Hindi / regional names for jewellery types

### Takeaway
Use bilingual labels. Common terms are kangan (rigid bangle), chudi (bangle), kada (thick bangle), jhumka (bell earring), bali (hoop), haar (necklace) and rani haar (long necklace), mangalsutra, nath (nose ring), maang tikka (forehead), bajuband (armlet), haath phool (hand ornament), kamarbandh (waist belt), payal (anklet) and bichhiya (toe ring; mettelu in Telugu, metti or minji in Tamil).

### Cited Findings
- Kangan is a rigid gold or silver bangle, often stone-set. Jhumka means "bell" earring. Haar and rani haar are necklaces. Payal (payaal) is the Hindi for anklet. Nath can be worn in either nostril and is often chained to the ear. Maang tikka is worn on the forehead at the hair parting. Sources: [CaratLane glossary](https://www.caratlane.com/jewellery/glossary.html), [GemPundit](https://www.gempundit.com/blog/types-of-indian-jewellery/), [whitegold.money](https://whitegold.money/types-of-gold-jewellery-india) (secondary), [Mia by Tanishq guide](https://www.miabytanishq.com/en/blogs/beginners-guide/classic-indian-jewellery-guide).
- A north Indian bridal set includes maang tikka, choker, rani haar, jhumka, bajuband, kangan and chudi, mangalsutra, haath phool, kamarbandh and payal. Source: [Lashkaraa guide](https://www.lashkaraa.com/blogs/lashkaraa/a-complete-guide-to-traditional-indian-jewelry-adornments) (secondary).
- Bichhiya is a toe ring worn by married Hindu women, usually silver and in pairs on the second toe. It is called mettelu in Telugu and minji in Tamil. Sources: [Wikipedia: Metti](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metti), [hindu-blog](https://www.hindu-blog.com/2020/04/bichiya-toe-ring-religious-significance.html), [Navbharat Live](https://navbharatlive.com/religion/silver-toe-rings-bichhiya-benefits-cultural-health-significance-rrs05) (secondary). GIVA uses "bichiya" in its own content: [GIVA adjustable bichiyas](https://www.giva.co/blogs/spotlight-on-silver/one-size-fits-all-discover-the-versatility-of-adjustable-silver-bichiyas).
- Tanishq sub-styles use Indian names directly: jhumka, bali (earrings), vanki (ring). Source: [Tanishq jewelry](https://www.tanishq.com/jewelry).
- Hindi–English ornament list: [samanyagyan](https://www.samanyagyan.com/eng/gk-jewels-and-ornaments-list) (secondary).

### Inferences
- Make search synonym-aware. Example mappings:
  - kangan / kada / chudi → bangles
  - jhumka / jhumki / bali / tops / kanser → earrings
  - haar / necklace / galsari → necklace
  - payal / pajeb / anklet → anklet
  - bichhiya / bichiya / metti / mettelu / toe ring → toe ring

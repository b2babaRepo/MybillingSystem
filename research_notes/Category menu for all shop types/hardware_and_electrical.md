# Category menus for Indian hardware/sanitary/paint shops and electrical shops

Method note: research done 2026-11-27 from web search result snippets only. Direct page fetches of moglix.com and industrybuying.com failed because those hosts are not allowed in this session's network settings, so category trees below are pieced together from category-page titles, URLs and search snippets, not from a full live menu read. Sources marked **(secondary)** are third-party blogs, vendor marketing comparisons, or aggregator snippets rather than the retailer/brand itself. Items marked **(own knowledge)** are not sourced and belong in the Gaps sections.

## 1. How do Indian B2B marketplaces, retailers and brand catalogues organise hardware/sanitary/paint and electrical categories?

### Takeaway
Moglix (the clearest example found) keeps Hardware, Fasteners, Plumbing and Electricals as separate top-level departments, each with a numeric category code and 2-3 levels below (e.g. Electricals > Wires & Cables > House Wires; Hardware > Door Hardware > Drawer Slides). Brand sites (Havells) split into consumer lines (Fans, Lighting, Switches, Switchgears, Appliances, Wires) and business lines. A shop menu modelled on this "department > category > sub-category" pattern, with fasteners and plumbing as their own departments, matches how Indian B2B buyers already browse.

## 2. Which filters matter (size, material, finish, brand, wattage, voltage, sq mm, colour/shade, pack/coil length, ISI, amps)?

### Takeaway
Variant attributes are written into product titles and drive filters: wires by sq mm + coil length + colour + insulation type (FR/FRLS/HR FR-LSH LF); switches by amps (6A/16A) + module size (1M/2M) + series; MCBs by pole + rating; pipes/fittings by mm with inch equivalent + SDR/class; hardware by inch length + material + finish + load; paint by pack size (1/4/10/20 L) + shade code.

## 3. How do counter staff find items in hardware/electrical POS (Marg, Busy, Vyapar, GoFrugal)?

### Takeaway
Indian billing products sell hardware features as: size/variant billing, unit conversion (piece to box, carton), unit-based pricing, barcode per product, and contractor credit ledgers. Documentation of quick size search ("1/2 inch elbow") or contractor price lists is thin; this is mostly marketing material.

### Cited Findings
- GoFrugal hardware POS: "maintain different prices based on the units your customers purchase", unique barcodes per product, billing with payables/receivables in one system. — [GoFrugal hardware shop software](https://www.gofrugal.com/retail/specialized-retail-pos/hardware-shop-software.html)
- GoFrugal HQ "Item Conversion Master" converts units (Pieces to Box, Pieces to Carton), under MDM > Conversion Master, shareable to outlets, with bulk conversion price update. — [GoFrugal community KB](https://community.gofrugal.com/portal/en/kb/articles/item-conversion-master-hq)
- Accountune: variant-wise billing, contractor credit ledger with limits and WhatsApp reminders; example that "GI pipe" is ten products spanning sizes, prices and GST rates. — [Accountune hardware (secondary, vendor marketing)](https://accountune.com/hardware-store-billing-software)
- GimBooks advertises variants (sizes, colours, batches) and SKU-based billing. — [GimBooks retail (secondary, vendor)](https://www.gimbooks.com/retail-billing-software)
- Comparison blogs recommend Vyapar, GoFrugal, GimBooks for small/medium hardware shops, Marg for large distributors; GoFrugal works offline with barcode scanners and many counters; Vyapar reported to struggle with multi-unit conversion at scale. — [GimBooks blog (secondary)](https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/hardware-shop-billing-software-in-2025/); [UdyogBill blog (secondary)](https://www.udyogbill.com/blog/best-hardware-billing-software-india-2026); [inzoo (secondary)](https://inzoo.in/guides/best-pos-software-in-india-2026)
- Vyapar and myBillBook run dedicated hardware-store billing pages. — [Vyapar hardware](https://vyaparapp.in/free/billing-software-for-retail-shop/hardware-stores); [myBillBook hardware](https://mybillbook.in/s/billing-software-for-hardware-store/)
- Brand price lists (Prince Pipes) give a per-size item code, which distributors and shops reuse in purchase orders. — [Prince price list](https://www.princepipes.com/pdf/Prince-Pipes-Pricelist-Pan-India-CPVC.pdf)

### Inferences
- Search should tokenise size forms: "1/2", "1/2 inch", "half inch", "15mm", "15 mm" should all match a 15 mm (1/2") item; "1.5 sq mm", "1.5mm", "1.5" for wire.
- Model parent item + variants (size, colour, pack) so the counter picks "Elbow CPVC" then a size chip, rather than scrolling 50 rows.
- Support customer-group price lists (retail / contractor / plumber / electrician) and brand-code lookups for supplier POs.

### Gaps
- No primary documentation found for Marg or Busy hardware item search, or for contractor price-list features in Vyapar/Marg/Busy.
- No first-hand source on how counter staff actually search (habits, shorthand).

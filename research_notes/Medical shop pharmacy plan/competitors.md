# Pharmacy software and apps used by Indian retail chemists (2026): competitors

Research date: 2026-11-03. Most pricing comes from resellers or aggregators, and much of the feature detail comes from vendors' own pages. Where sources conflict, both are noted.

## 1. Main products: platform, price and pharmacy features

### Takeaway
The market splits into four groups. Desktop or chain ERPs (Marg, GoFrugal, Medeil, LOGIC) are deep but heavy, sold through dealers and priced opaquely. Pharmacy SaaS tools (eVitalRx, Accountune) are cloud-based and cheaper. Generic mobile billing apps (Vyapar, myBillBook) are cheap and mobile but shallow for pharmacy. Ledger and payment apps (OkCredit, Khatabook, BharatPe) handle only udhaar. Separately, B2B ordering apps (Retailio) and quick commerce or e-pharmacy shape what chemists buy and who they sell to.

### Cited Findings
**Marg ERP 9+ (pharma retail and distribution)**
- No official public pricing; it is quoted by dealers. Reseller one-time licences run ₹8,100 (Basic, single user) to ₹13,899 (Pharma Distribution) — [erpresearch/itforsme summary](https://itforsme.in/pricing/marg-erp-india); [IndiaMART listing](https://m.indiamart.com/totalpharma-solutions/billing-software.html)
- An annual licence of ₹15,600/year exists for some editions (supermarket edition) — [IndiaMART](https://m.indiamart.com/city/thane/marg-application-software-packages.html)
- One-time ₹8,100–₹25,200 plus about ₹3,500/year AMC; desktop-first with cloud as an add-on. Features: batch and expiry, schemes, drug licence tracking, built-in drug database, barcode billing. Called overkill for a single-counter chemist. Source is a competitor, Accountune — [Accountune](https://accountune.com/best-billing-software-medical-store-india)
- Reviews: customer support rated 2.9 on Capterra (50 reviews); overall 3.2/5 from 54 reviews on Software Advice. GetApp's summary says many users report slow, unresponsive after-sales support. A Trustpilot chemist reviewer called it "very very hard and slow to operate". A pharma user on Capterra titled their May 2025 review "dont buy marg" — [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/sales-software/a/marg-erp-9/); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/business-management/marg-erp-9-plus-profile/); [Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/margbooks.com); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/151832/Erp-Software-9/)

**GoFrugal (pharmacy edition)**
- Cloud and on-premise, built for chains. Features: batch and expiry, multi-store control, low-stock and expiry alerts, e-invoicing. Pricing on request — [Accountune](https://accountune.com/best-billing-software-medical-store-india)
- Price data conflicts. TrustRadius lists Starter $375/yr, Standard $625/yr, Professional $1,500/yr. TechImply lists INR 18,000 / 36,000 / 85,000 (labelled "/month", probably a mislabel) and says there is no free trial — [TrustRadius](https://tr-web.dev.trustradius.com/compare-products/bestrx-vs-gofrugal-pharmacy-management-software); [TechImply](https://www.techimply.com/profile/gofrugal-pharmacy-software)
- GoFrugal's blog discusses AI predictive analytics for demand forecasting in general terms, not as a named shipped feature — [GoFrugal blog](https://www.gofrugal.com/blog/retail-pharmacy-trends)

**Medeil**
- Starts at $90.50 per user per year, with a free trial — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/117249/Medeil/); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/retail/medeil-profile)
- Cloud and on-premise, leaning toward hospitals: prescription-linked dispensing, ward supply, multi-outlet — [Accountune](https://accountune.com/best-billing-software-medical-store-india)

**eMedStore**
- I found no pricing or feature data. It appears only as an alternative listed on TechImply — [TechImply](https://www.techimply.com/profile/gofrugal-pharmacy-software)

**eVitalRx (cloud pharmacy SaaS, adjacent competitor)**
- Claims 20,000+ pharmacists. Sells on ONDC, Amazon, Flipkart and WhatsApp. Suggests in-stock generics by salt composition. Integrates with Tally, Pharmarack, Paytm and EMR (Eka Care) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1637429769)
- Sends invoices and payment links on WhatsApp; offers and reminders on WhatsApp — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/evitalrx)
- Price reported as about ₹3,500/year in one source and $43/year in another. Reviews are thin: 3.0 from 2 App Store ratings, plus a 1/5 review on TrustRadius ("worst software purchase") — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/evitalrx); [TrustRadius](https://web-v2.prod.trustradius.com/products/evitalrx-pharmacy-software/reviews)
- Purchase entry reads supplier bills — [Accountune](https://accountune.com/best-billing-software-medical-store-india)

**Vyapar (generic, used by pharmacies)**
- Mobile-first on Android, with offline mode; desktop is sold separately. Its medical-shop page advertises batch and expiry, "Schedule H / H1 Record Ready", and expiry shown on invoices — [Vyapar medical shop page](https://vyaparapp.in/free/billing-software-for-medical-shop)
- A competitor says Vyapar's batch and expiry handling is limited, it has no drug database, and HSN codes must be entered manually — [Accountune](https://accountune.com/best-billing-software-medical-store-india)
- The live page showed paid plans at ₹0, which looks like a rendering error, so the actual price was not verified.

**myBillBook (generic)**
- Batch and expiry tracking for medicines — [myBillBook KB](https://knowledge.mybillbook.in/articles/4727101-how-to-use-batching-in-mybillbook)
- Price conflicts: plans from ₹399/yr on its own page, versus from ₹291/month per a third party, versus an older Enterprise plan at ₹417/month billed annually plus GST — [myBillBook blog](https://mybillbook.in/blog/medical-invoicing-software-with-batch-tracking/); [itsupplychain](https://itsupplychain.com/10-best-billing-software-for-retail-shops-in-india-2026/)

**Accountune (cloud-only competitor; author of a comparison cited above)**
- Free plan, with paid plans from ₹799/year. Web, Android and iOS. Batch, expiry alerts, HSN lookup, udhaar. No scheme or distribution features and no dispensing module — [Accountune](https://accountune.com/best-billing-software-medical-store-india)

**OkCredit / Khatabook / BharatPe (ledger only)**
- OkCredit says medical and pharmacy stores are among its primary users. It offers monthly patient accounts, repeat-medicine credit and automatic reminders, and reports more than 5M monthly active users — [OkCredit medical store page](https://okcredit.in/khata-app-for-medical-store); [OkCredit FAQ](https://okcredit.in/faq)
- Competitors with a digital-ledger feature include Khatabook, Vyapar, BharatPe, PhonePe and Paytm — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=169469)

**Retailio (B2B ordering, linked to PharmEasy)**
- Pune-based, founded 2017. Its retailer app offers smart reorder recommendations, deals, real-time stock, integration with distributor ERPs, and UPI or netbanking payment of outstanding invoices — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1245047831)
- Embedded pay-later credit through Indifi (2021) — [Indian Startup Times](https://www.indianstartuptimes.com/?p=23924)
- Listed as PharmEasy's B2B distribution arm — [Pocketful case study](https://www.pocketful.in/blog/pharmaeasy-case-study/)

**Quick commerce and e-pharmacy (competition for chemists)**
- AIOCD has asked for a ban on 10-minute prescription drug delivery by Zepto, Blinkit and Swiggy-PharmEasy — [Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/news/industry/pharma/chemists-body-warns-of-55-rise-in-drug-abuse-calls-for-ban-on-10-minute-prescription-drug-delivery-153461); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/industry/story/can-cause-serious-health-threats-swiggy-pharmeasys-10-minute-medicine-delivery-sparks-alarm-among-chemists-association-452374-2024-11-03)
- AIOCD called a nationwide strike on 20 May 2026 against e-pharmacies, and the response was mixed — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/health/pharmacies-across-india-shut-may-20-epharmacy-concerns-enn26051305103)
- Flipkart Minutes says it will fulfil through licensed local pharmacies, with the platform handling only logistics — [Third Eyesight](https://www.thirdeyesight.in/?p=6611)
- A recent (Oct 2026) debate covers 10-minute delivery with "30-second" online prescriptions — [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/10-minutes-delivery-and-30-second-rx-quick-commerce-platforms-deliver-medicines-triggering-prescription-on-demand-debate)

### Inferences
- Pricing tiers: about ₹0–4k/yr (mobile apps and SaaS), about ₹8–25k one-time plus AMC (Marg), and quote-based chain ERPs (GoFrugal, Medeil).
- Quick commerce is both a threat and an opening. Platforms routing orders to local licensed chemists (Flipkart Minutes, ONDC) create demand for software that can take those orders. eVitalRx already does this.

### Gaps
- No pricing or reviews found for eMedStore. No current-year Vyapar pharmacy price. No Mstar data. Nothing specific to BharatPe or Khatabook for chemists.
- Play Store review text was not mined directly. No YouTube transcripts were reviewed.
- Salt substitutes, H1 register and purchase import were confirmed only where noted above.

## 2. What chemists value most, and what is missing

### Takeaway
The recurring pain points in the evidence are hard-to-use desktop ERPs with poor after-sales support (Marg), and generic mobile apps that lack pharmacy depth: drug database, HSN codes, batch handling (Vyapar). Chemists need batch and expiry tracking, GST, H1, generics and udhaar, without dealer lock-in.

### Cited Findings
- Marg's main complaints are poor support and difficult operation — [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/sales-software/a/marg-erp-9/); [Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/margbooks.com)
- Marg is described as more system than a single-counter shop needs; GoFrugal is built for chains, so single shops pay for features they don't use — [Accountune](https://accountune.com/best-billing-software-medical-store-india)
- Vyapar lacks a drug database and has limited batch handling — same source
- OkCredit's medical-store pitch centres on credit for repeat medicines and reminders that replace awkward payment calls — [OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/khata-app-for-medical-store)
- Established desktop systems typically cost ₹15,000–₹50,000 one-time (vendor-authored blog) — [Codingclave](https://codingclave.com/blog/best-pharmacy-software-india-2026)

### Inferences
- An opening exists: a mobile-first app in Hindi and English combining (a) pharmacy depth (batch, expiry, H1 register, a salt and generic database with HSN pre-filled), (b) udhaar with WhatsApp reminders, and (c) purchase-bill import. No single competitor found combines all three for a single-counter shop at under about ₹2k/yr.
- Self-serve onboarding with no dealer directly counters Marg's support complaints.

### Gaps
- No quantitative survey of chemist feature priorities was found.
- Hindi-language UI support per vendor was not verified.

## 3. AI features already shipped

### Takeaway
I found no confirmed AI launches from Marg, GoFrugal, Medeil, Vyapar or myBillBook. Closest to AI: eVitalRx reads supplier bills and suggests generics by salt, and Retailio makes reorder recommendations. Prescription scanning appears only in small startups and hackathon projects.

### Cited Findings
- eVitalRx suggests generics by salt composition (rule-based, not stated as AI) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1637429769); its purchase entry reads supplier bills — [Accountune](https://accountune.com/best-billing-software-medical-store-india)
- Retailio offers "smart" reorder recommendations — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1245047831)
- GoFrugal discusses AI forecasting only generically — [GoFrugal blog](https://www.gofrugal.com/blog/retail-pharmacy-trends)
- PharmEasy reportedly uses AI for demand forecasting internally (low-authority source); IndiaAI describes AI-driven customer engagement through CleverTap — [IndiaAI](https://indiaai.gov.in/article/pharmeasy-s-ai-and-analytics-advances-online-healthcare)
- Startup Nesayo lists a Claude Vision prescription scan at ₹499/month (self-reported) — [hunted.space](https://hunted.space/product/nesayo); a hackathon project reads handwritten prescriptions into bills — [lablab.ai](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/amd-developer/wtf-ninja/aushadhi-vault)

### Inferences
- AI prescription-to-bill, distributor-invoice photo or PDF to purchase entry, expiry and dead-stock forecasting, and a WhatsApp ordering bot are all still largely open among mainstream vendors.

### Gaps
- Vendor release notes from 2025–26 were not checked directly, so absence of evidence is not proof that no AI features exist.

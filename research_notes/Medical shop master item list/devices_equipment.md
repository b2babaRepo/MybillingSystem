# Medical devices, surgical items and home-care equipment (India, 2026)

Scope note: Only one web search was run, so the evidence base is thin. The search covered the GST rates and HSN codes. Product names and brands come from general market knowledge of Indian chemist and surgical shops; no individual SKU was checked against a website. The report writer should treat brand–product pairs as typical, not verified listings. Where the GST rate is unsure it says "5?" or "unknown". In the HSN column, "?" means the heading is likely but not confirmed.

## GST and HSN basis for medical devices after GST 2.0 (22 Sept 2025)

### Takeaway
The sources say most Chapter 90 medical, diagnostic and orthopaedic items (HSN 9018, 9019, 9021) dropped to 5% from 22 Sept 2025. They also say glucometers and test strips are 5% under Notification 9/2025-CT(Rate). The rates for thermometers, bandages, gloves, diapers, rubber hot-water bags and household scales were NOT confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Blood glucose monitoring systems and test strips are listed at 5% from 22 Sept 2025, citing Notification 9/2025-Central Tax (Rate), under HSN 90189019 / "90 or any other Chapter" — [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/hsn-code/90189019)
- HSN 9018 (medical, surgical, dental instruments) is summarised at 5% under GST 2.0, effective 22 Sept 2025 — [Mark IT Solutions](https://markitsolutions-website.pages.dev/hsn/9018); also [Patron Accounting 90181990](https://www.patronaccounting.com/hsn-code/90181990)
- HSN 9019 (mechano-therapy and massage apparatus) and 9021 (orthopaedic and assistive devices) are listed at 5% — [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/learn/gst-on-medicines/)
- One site lists oxygen therapy apparatus (9019 20) and pacemakers (9021 50) at 0%. Razorpay lists the wider headings at 5%, and the 0% claim was not verified — [Disytax](https://disytax.com/hsn-chapter-90-optical-medical-instruments-gst/)
- General overviews of GST 2.0 say the rates on medicines and medical devices were cut — [MedBound Times](https://www.medboundtimes.com/amp/story/daily-pulse/gst-2-0-healthcare-medicines-insurance-affordable-india); [Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/health-education-gst-rates/)

### Inferences
- In the app, it is reasonably safe to default items under 9018, 9019, 9021, 3005 and 3822 to 5%. Every other item should carry an "unverified" flag that the shopkeeper confirms against the CBIC schedule.
- Weighing scales (8423) and general household plastics are probably still at 18% or 12% and were not part of the medical cuts. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- No primary CBIC schedule was read. Rates for 9025 thermometers, 4015 gloves, 9619 diapers and pads, 4014 rubber hot-water and ice bags, 6307 masks, 3924 plastic bedpans and urinals, and 8713 wheelchairs are not confirmed.
- Pack sizes and brand availability were not checked against live listings (1mg, Netmeds, brand sites).

### Inferences
- The orthopaedic range sold by Tynor, Vissco and Flamingo looks largely interchangeable, so the app could store one generic item ("Knee cap") with the brand as a variant attribute instead of a separate row for each brand.
- The water-related items fall under widely varying HSN headings (4014 rubber, 3922 plastic, 9018 medical, 8516 electric), so their GST is the least certain group in the list.

### Gaps
- The brand–product pairs, sizes and pack units were not each checked against a brand site or e-pharmacy listing; they are typical market knowledge.
- The following rates are unconfirmed, and each is marked 5?, 18? or unknown in the table: 9025 thermometers, 4015 gloves, 9619 diapers, 6307 masks, 4014 hot-water and ice bags, 8516 vaporisers, 8423 scales, spirit, and hydrogen peroxide.
- The claimed 0% rate on oxygen therapy apparatus (9019 20) was not verified.

# Wellness, Ayurveda, sexual wellness and baby care at Indian chemists (master item list, 2026)

Scope note: this was a short pass with one web search. Product names and brands come from general market knowledge of what Indian chemists widely stock. Pack sizes are common SKUs, but they change often, so check them against current packs before seeding the database. GST rates follow GST 2.0 (in force from 22 Sep 2025) as far as one search could confirm. Check every rate marked "?" against the CBIC rate notification before invoicing.

## GST and HSN basis

### Takeaway
After GST 2.0, most chemist wellness items are at 5%: Ayurvedic and allopathic medicaments (3004), toothpaste, talc, soap, hair oil and shampoo, diapers and baby products. Condoms and sanitary pads are believed to be nil. The rate for health drinks and nutrition powders is not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- GST 2.0 rates took effect on 22 Sep 2025, after the 56th GST Council meeting on 3 Sep 2025 — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/tax/story/big-gst-cuts-from-sept-22-see-whats-cheaper-now-and-what-got-pricier-full-breakdown-inside-492411-2025-09-03); [Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/new-gst-rates-september-2025-business-impact/)
- Shampoo, talcum powder, toothpaste, toothbrushes, face powder, soap and hair oil were cut from 18% to 5% — [Organiser](https://organiser.org/2025/09/04/314039/bharat/gst-reforms-2-0-new-slabs-implementation-date-and-what-gets-cheaper-all-you-need-to-know/); [Cleartax comparison](https://cleartax.in/s/new-vs-old-gst-rates-comparison)
- Feeding bottles, baby napkins and clinical diapers went from 12% to 5% — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/new-gst-rate-cut-what-gets-cheaper-expensive-5-18-percent-125092200215_1.html)
- Medicaments, including Ayurvedic, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathic ones, are in the 5% slab — [Cleartax](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-rates)
- Cleartax lists contraceptives and condoms among nil or zero-rated items. Only a snippet was seen, and no second source confirmed it — [Cleartax](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-rates)
- The WB update shows tooth powder moving from 12% to 5%, so sources disagree on the earlier rates — [WB Commercial Tax](https://comtax.wb.gov.in/GST/GST_PPU/Monthly%20GST%20Updates[Part%20B]_Rate_September%202025_20251008.pdf)

### Inferences
- Sanitary pads, tampons and menstrual cups (HSN 9619) have been exempt since July 2018. This comes from background knowledge and was not re-verified here.
- Bandages and plasters (3005) and pregnancy test kits (3822) were at 12% before GST 2.0. They are probably 5% now, but this is unverified.
- Emergency contraceptives (levonorgestrel 1.5 mg) are 3004 medicaments, so probably 5%. Their schedule status (the brief says Schedule H) and OTC practice need checking.

### Gaps
- The CBIC notification itself was not read. Rates still to confirm: health drinks and nutrition powders (1901/2106), glucose powders (1702), lubricants, menstrual cups, gripe water and wipes.
- Pack sizes were not checked against 2026 brand catalogues.

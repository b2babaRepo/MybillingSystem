# Skin care products for the beauty shop master item list

## GST rate and HSN for skin care after the 22 Sept 2025 GST changes

### Takeaway
From 22 Sept 2025, talcum powder, face powder (HSN 3304 91), toilet soap bars (3401), hair oil, shampoo, toothpaste and shaving cream/after-shave moved from 18% to 5%. Skin care products stayed at 18%: face creams, moisturisers, sunscreens, serums, lip balms, lotions, gels, bleach, face packs and facial kits (3304 99 / 3304 10).

### Cited Findings
- Shampoo, talcum powder, toothpaste, toothbrushes, face powder, soap and hair oil were cut to 5% from 18% — [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/tax/new-gst-rates-full-list-of-items-with-5-goods-and-services-tax/article-180697); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2025/09/gst-council-overhaul-cuts-tax-everyday-essentials)
- Talcum powder, face powder, shaving cream and after-shave lotion moved from 18% to 5% — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/new-gst-rate-cut-what-gets-cheaper-expensive-5-18-percent-125092200215_1.html)
- An HSN table lists talcum/face powder (3304) and toilet soap (3401) at 5% — [MS Associates](https://www.msassociates.pro/gst/gst-personal-care-and-hygiene.php)
- Face cream, sunscreen, moisturising lotion, serum and lip balm saw no rate change, so they stay at 18% — [Khaitan & Co](https://khaitanco.com/thought-leadership/GST-Rate-Changes-and-impact-under-Regulatory-laws)
- Nuvama said skin care and cosmetics saw no change — [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/stocks/gst-2-0-gst-cuts-elude-these-key-items-analysts-and-fmcg-players-say-it-is-surprising/article-180835)
- The change took effect 22 Sept 2025, and invoices up to 21 Sept used the old rates — [Newsonair](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/gst-council-approves-major-rate-cuts-effective-september-22); [StudyCafe](https://studycafe.in/fmcg-majors-issue-trade-notices-as-gst-reduced-on-household-essentials-393148.html)

### Inferences
- Default for skin care rows: GST 18, HSN 3304 99. Lip products: 3304 10. Petroleum jelly (Vaseline Original): 2712 at 18%. Antiseptic or ayurvedic creams (Boroplus, Boroline, Vicco) may be filed by the maker as ayurvedic medicament (3004, 5%?), so they are marked "?".
- Liquid or gel face wash (3401 30 or 3304 99) is marked "18?". The 5% cut was reported for toilet soap in bar or cake form, and I found no source that clearly extends it to liquid face wash. Check the GST rate printed on the supplier's invoice.

### Gaps
- I did not read the exact notification text for 3401 30 (liquid skin-washing products). It needs a check against CBIC Notification 9/2025-CT(Rate).
- Pack sizes come from general market knowledge and were not checked product by product on Nykaa or Amazon. Brands change sizes often, so treat sizes as a starting point.

# Beauty shop master item list: makeup and nails

Method note: the seed rows below come from general knowledge of the Indian retail range: brand product lines as seen on Nykaa, Purplle, Amazon.in and brand sites. Only one web search was run, and it was for GST. Product names and pack sizes have not been checked against live listings one by one. Packs marked "?" are uncertain. Brands change shades, pack sizes and sub-lines often, so check against a distributor price list before shipping.

## GST and HSN after the 22 Sept 2025 rate changes

### Takeaway
After GST 2.0 (56th Council meeting on 3-4 Sept 2025, in force from 22 Sept 2025), talcum powder and face powder (HSN 3304 91) moved from 18% to 5%. All other makeup stays at 18%. That covers lip makeup (3304 10), eye makeup (3304 20), nail preparations (3304 30) and other makeup such as foundation, primer, BB cream and blush (3304 99).

### Cited Findings
- A tax-practice table lists HSN 3304 talcum powder and face powder at 18% before 22.09.2025 and 5% on or after that date. Shampoo and hair oil (3305), toothpaste (3306) and toilet soap (3401) also moved to 5% — [MS Associates](https://www.msassociates.pro/gst/gst-personal-care-and-hygiene.php); also see [TaxTMI](https://www.taxtmi.com/article/detailed?id=15061)
- The 56th GST Council meeting took place on 3-4 Sept 2025, and the new rates took effect on 22 Sept 2025 — [TaxTMI blog](https://www.taxtmi.com/tmi_blog_details?id=849121)
- The 8-digit codes come from HSN lookup pages: 33041000 lip, 33042000 eye, 33043000 nail, 33049110 face powder, 33049120 talcum, 33049990 other — [Credlix 330491](https://www.credlix.com/hsn-code/330491), [Credlix 330430](https://www.credlix.com/hsn-code/330430), [Credlix 330420](https://www.credlix.com/hsn-code/330420). One IncorpX page still shows 28% because it uses the 2017 rate mapping, so treat it as out of date — [IncorpX](https://www.incorpx.io/hsn-codes/code-33049120)

### Inferences
- Compact powder and loose/setting powder are "face powder", so they should fall under 33049110 at 5%. Some brands may still bill pressed compacts at 18% under 3304 99. Mark these 5%? until checked against a brand invoice.
- Rates for the makeup tools are my best guess, not sourced:
  - Brushes: 9603 29, 18%?
  - Sponges: 3926 90 / 4016, 18%?
  - False lashes: 6704, 18%?
  - Lash glue: 3506, 18%?
  - Artificial nails: 3926 90, 18%?
  - Makeup remover wipes: 3307 90 / 3401, 18%?
- Nail polish remover is a nail preparation, so 33043000 at 18%.

### Gaps
- I did not read the official CBIC notification (9/2025-CT(Rate)) directly.
- I have not confirmed whether pressed compact powder is billed under 3304 91 at 5% in practice.

### Inferences
- Shopkeepers often sell nail polish and kajal "by shade". The app should model shade as a variant, not as separate rows.
- Mixed kits: bill them at the highest rate among their contents (composite or mixed supply). Treat this as 18% unless every item in the kit is a 5% item.

### Gaps
- Pack sizes for small brands (Insight, Blue Heaven, Elle 18, Mars, Renee) were not checked against listings, so they are marked "?".
- HSN for the non-cosmetic tools (brushes, lashes, glue, artificial nails) is a best guess.

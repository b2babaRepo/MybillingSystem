# Shop subscription plans, offers, complimentary access, hold, and payment collection (India SMB SaaS, 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-30. Prices change often and most Indian vendors show heavy "strike-through" discounts, so treat every figure as a snapshot. Prices exclude 18% GST unless stated.

## 1. Competitor plans, prices, durations, free tiers, trials, device limits

### Takeaway
The Indian billing/khata market sells on **1-year and 3-year prepaid terms**, not monthly. 3-year plans cost about **2.0-2.5x the 1-year price** (roughly a 25-40% discount). Mobile-only tiers cost ₹400-600/yr, desktop+mobile ₹2,400-3,000/yr. Khata-first apps (Khatabook, OkCredit) keep the core ledger free. I found no 5-year or lifetime plans from the main vendors.

### Cited Findings

Saver **3-year** Desktop+Mobile ₹6,299 (₹10,999); Desktop only ₹5,499 (₹8,999). Mobile-only: 1-year ₹599 (₹899), 3-year ₹1,349 (₹2,699). There is no monthly plan, and the page lists no lifetime price even though a heading mentions "lifetime". — [Vyapar pricing](https://vyaparapp.in/index.php/pricing)
- On Vyapar the 3-year price works out to 2.25x the 1-year price (₹6,299 vs ₹2,799), or 2.25x for mobile (₹1,349 vs ₹599). — derived from [Vyapar pricing](https://vyaparapp.in/index.php/pricing)

### Inferences
- Your planned ladder (monthly / yearly / 3-year / 5-year) is broader than the market. Kirana buyers are used to **annual or 3-year one-time payments**. Monthly mainly works if it is cheap and on UPI AutoPay. 5-year has no clear precedent among the leaders.
- Price anchors for a kirana PWA: about ₹400-600/yr is mobile-only entry, about ₹2,500-3,000/yr is the "full" tier. A 3-year price near 2.2x annual matches what the market expects.
- Strike-through MRP plus a "% off" badge is standard presentation in this market.
- A free core ledger is the norm for khata apps (Khatabook/OkCredit), so charging for the basic udhaar ledger would cut against market expectations.

### Gaps
- I could not get official current prices for Khatabook premium, Cashbook, OkCredit, Petpooja or myBillBook's top tiers.
- Device limits are only explicit for myBillBook Silver (1 device). Vyapar's device rules were not stated on its page.
- I found no evidence of 5-year or lifetime plans from these vendors. Recheck before claiming none exist.

## 2. Mechanics: trials, coupons/referrals, complimentary accounts, grace, expiry, pause, proration, refunds

### Takeaway
Public evidence on post-expiry behaviour is thin for Indian vendors. The common pattern elsewhere in accounting software is a grace period followed by **read-only access with data retained**. Trials of 7-14 days, referral codes, sales-assisted multi-year discounts and dealer/partner channels are the main levers.

### Cited Findings
- myBillBook offers a 14-day free trial and sales-negotiated multi-year discounts of up to 35%. — [myBillBook](https://freetrial.mybillbook.in/silver-plan-for-android.html)
- BUSY offers a "free demo" and a permanent free tier (Express). Plan upgrades and migrations carry over the remaining validity. — [BUSY pricing](https://busy.in/pricing.md)
- Marg and BUSY sell through dealer/partner networks that set their own prices. This is the Indian precedent for agent-led sales and offline collection. — [ERPResearch Marg](https://www.erpresearch.com/pricing/marg-erp)
- Expiry patterns from comparable accounting software: TaxCalc's database becomes read-only on lapse; IRIS gives 45 days of normal access and then switches to read-only; MacPractice gives 6 months free read-only; Reckon users can still view data but cannot add entries. — [TaxCalc](https://kb.taxcalc.com/2699); [IRIS](https://www.iris.co.uk/support/knowledgebase/kb/pt-bt-p11d-vat-trust-tax-read-only-mode/); [MacPractice](https://macpractice.atlassian.net/wiki/x/2oBaBQ); [Reckon](https://community.reckon.com/discussion/comment/20616024)
- Razorpay Subscriptions supports trial periods, upfront charges, add-ons, and customer-side **upgrade, downgrade, pause, cancel**. Pausing is available from the Dashboard. Its overview page does not document the proration rules. — [Razorpay Subscriptions](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/subscriptions/)

### Inferences
- Recommended model for Grocery Khata: a trial (7-14 days), then a grace period (for example 7-15 days, full access with banners), then **read-only** (khata, bills and export still viewable, no new paid-feature entries). Never delete data while it is still within retention.
- A complimentary plan should be an internal entitlement grant (₹0, with an end date, a reason and the granting admin recorded), not a fake payment. **Hold/suspend** should be a separate admin state from "expired" so that read-only or locked behaviour stays deliberate and auditable.
- For proration on upgrades, the simplest approach that matches BUSY is to **convert the remaining validity into credit or extra days** on the new plan, rather than doing cash proration.

### Gaps
- I found no published Vyapar, myBillBook or Khatabook policy on expiry behaviour, grace length or refunds.
- I found no Indian vendor's documented "pause subscription" feature for shops, beyond what Razorpay supports.

## 3. Multi-year (3/5-year) prepaid plans: prevalence and risks

### Takeaway
3-year prepaid is common and well accepted (Vyapar Saver, myBillBook multi-year). 5-year plans are rare. The main risks are GST being due upfront on the full advance, revenue that must be deferred over the term, and refund or price-lock liability if the product or pricing changes.

### Cited Findings
- Vyapar sells 3-year "Saver" plans at about 2.25x the annual price. — [Vyapar pricing](https://vyaparapp.in/index.php/pricing)
- For services under GST, the time of supply is the **earlier of invoice or receipt of payment** (CGST Act s.13). GST is therefore payable when an advance is received, unlike advances for goods, which are exempt under Notification 66/2017. — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/advance-received-under-gst); [Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/when-is-gst-payable-on-advance-payments-rules-for-goods-vs-services/); [GST Council flyer](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/e-version-gst-flyers/51_GST_Flyer_Chapter6.pdf)

### Inferences
- If a shop prepays a 5-year plan, you pay GST on the full amount immediately, while revenue under Ind AS 115 / AS 9 is recognised over 60 months. That leaves a 5-year deferred-revenue liability, and refunds on early closure become complicated (credit notes, GST reversal).
- Mitigations: cap multi-year plans at 3 years for v1, or sell 5-year as a "price lock" with annual billing; publish a clear no-refund or pro-rata refund policy; make terms say that features may change but core khata access is guaranteed for the term.

### Gaps
- I found no authoritative source on Ind AS 115 treatment specific to Indian SaaS multi-year plans. A CA should confirm.

## 4. Payments in India: e-mandate rules, UPI AutoPay, gateways, manual UPI/cash, GST invoices

### Takeaway
The RBI's 2026 E-Mandate Framework keeps the **₹15,000 per-debit limit without extra authentication** (₹1 lakh for insurance, mutual funds and credit card bills), requires a **pre-debit notice at least 24 hours ahead**, and lets customers cancel anytime with no registration fee. Every plan Grocery Khata would charge sits well below ₹15,000, so UPI AutoPay can renew it silently after the first authenticated setup.

### Cited Findings
- The 2021 framework required AFA on the first transaction. The June 2022 circular raised the no-AFA limit for later debits from ₹5,000 to ₹15,000. — [RBI notification 12341](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12341); [Mondaq](https://mondaq.com/india/financial-services/1206696/rbi-enhances-transaction-limits-for-processing-of-e-mandates-for-recurring-transactions-from-inr-5000-to-inr-15000-)
- **Digital Payments - E-Mandate Framework, 2026 (RBI, April 2026):** a single direction for cards, UPI and PPIs. The first debit needs AFA. Later debits up to ₹15,000 need none (₹1,00,000 for insurance, MF and credit card bills); above that, dynamic AFA is needed per debit. The issuer must send a pre-debit notice ≥24 hours ahead with merchant, amount, date and reference. Customers can opt out of a single debit or cancel anytime. No charge may be levied for registration or use. — [IAS Gyan explainer](https://www.iasgyan.in/daily-current-affairs/rbis-e-mandate-framework-2026-explained); [IANS, 21 Apr 2026](https://ianslive.in/rbi-tightens-e-mandate-rules-makes-extra-authentication-mandatory-for-recurring-payments-above-rs-15000--20260421192243). These are secondary sources; I did not read the RBI master direction text itself.
- Under the UPI AutoPay process, NPCI moved recurring debits into fixed time slots from 1 Aug 2025. One blog reports a maximum of 1 attempt plus 3 retries (single source, low confidence). Mandate limits vary by MCC category. — [Sakshi Post](https://www.sakshipost.com/news/technology/new-upi-rules-august-1-daily-limits-and-autopay-changes-explained-432540); [Razorpay UPI recurring](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-gateway/s2s-integration/recurring-payments/upi)
- UPI AutoPay volumes doubled by late 2025. — [Vision IAS / ET](https://visionias.in/current-affairs/upsc-daily-news-summary/article/2025-12-26/the-economic-times/economy/upi-autopay-volumes-double-on-ease-enhanced-limit)
- Razorpay Subscriptions supports cards (tokenised), UPI AutoPay (PhonePe, GPay, Paytm, BHIM) and eMandate (Aadhaar, netbanking, debit card). It creates an invoice per cycle, retries failed payments automatically, and supports trials, add-ons, pause and plan changes. — [Razorpay Subscriptions](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/subscriptions/)
- Cashfree offers UPI AutoPay; the approved mandate amount caps what can be collected. — [Cashfree UPI AutoPay](https://www.cashfree.com/en/upi-autopay/)
- PhonePe PG compares UPI AutoPay, e-NACH and cards for subscriptions. — [PhonePe Business](https://business.phonepe.com/articles/how-to-accept-subscription-payments-in-india-upi-auto-pay-vs-e-nach-vs-cards)

### Inferences
- Support three collection paths, all ending in one "payment record" and one GST invoice:
  1. A gateway subscription (UPI AutoPay or card) for monthly and annual plans.
  2. A one-time UPI/QR or payment link for 3-year and 5-year prepaid plans. The amount may exceed ₹15,000 later, but a one-off payment does not need a mandate.
  3. **Manual or agent cash/UPI entry** by an admin, with receipt number, agent and proof photo. This matches the Marg/BUSY dealer channel.
- Every payment needs a GST tax invoice (or receipt voucher for advances) with SAC 998431/997331-type coding and GSTIN if the shop has one. A CA should confirm the SAC code.
- Do not rely on silent retries. Show your own reminder before the bank's 24-hour notice, and drop into the grace state on failure.

### Gaps
- I did not verify the RBI 2026 master direction text itself (effective date, exact wording).
- I did not fetch Razorpay or Cashfree subscription fees or proration specifics.

## 5. Feature gating and entitlement models

### Takeaway
Gate value-add features (multi-device, GST reports, inventory, voice/OCR AI, bulk reminders, multiple shops), not the shop's own records. Market leaders keep the basic khata free (Khatabook, OkCredit, BUSY Express, Zoho Free), and expired accounts elsewhere stay viewable read-only.

### Cited Findings
- Free core tiers exist: BUSY Express (free billing), Zoho Books Free (capped by turnover, users and invoices), free Khatabook and OkCredit ledgers. — [BUSY](https://busy.in/pricing.md); [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/zoho-books-pricing-india-2026); [Business Scroll](https://www.thebusinessscroll.com/khatabook-business-model/)
- Gating axes vendors actually use: device count and platform (myBillBook Silver: 1 device, Android only); users and companies (BUSY POWER+ 10 users, 150 companies); invoice volume and turnover (Zoho Free); add-on credits (BUSY Mobile App Lite, 250 credits). — [myBillBook](https://freetrial.mybillbook.in/silver-plan-for-android.html); [BUSY](https://busy.in/pricing.md)
- Read-only on expiry is the common pattern across accounting software. — [TaxCalc](https://kb.taxcalc.com/2699); [IRIS](https://www.iris.co.uk/support/knowledgebase/kb/pt-bt-p11d-vat-trust-tax-read-only-mode/)

### Inferences
- **Entitlement model:** plan → a set of feature flags and limits (devices, users, AI credits/month). Then add per-shop overrides (admin add or remove a feature, complimentary grant) with start and end dates and an audit log. Compute an "effective entitlements" view at request time, and cache it offline in the PWA with an expiry timestamp.
- **Always free, never locked:** viewing and exporting the khata/udhaar, viewing past bills, the customer list, and data export. Even in suspend state, allow read and export unless the suspension is for fraud or abuse.
- **Usage-based items** (AI voice/OCR) fit better as monthly credit quotas than as hard plan gates.

### Gaps
- I found no published entitlement or flag documentation from Indian khata vendors. The model above is synthesis, not cited practice.

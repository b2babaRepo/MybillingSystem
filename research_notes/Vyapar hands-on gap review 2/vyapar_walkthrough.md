# Vyapar desktop walkthrough (look-only, partial)

Date: 2026-11-17. The company is new ("Enter Business Name"), with 6 days of free trial left. The walkthrough stopped early because the user interrupted it.

## Home / first-sale wizard
- "Enter details to make your first Sale" opens on Home and takes over most of the left-menu clicks (Sale, Reports and Settings all landed back on it).
- Invoice Details: Invoice Number (01), Invoice Date. Bill To: Customer Name*. "Add Sample Item" button.
- Invoice Calculation: Invoice Amount*, Received, Balance. Button: "Create Your First Invoice".
- A sample Tax Invoice preview sits on the right (Bill To, Invoice Details, items table #/Item name/Qty/Price/Unit/GST/Amt, Amount in Words, Sub Total, SGST@9%, CGST@9%, Total, Balance Due).
- Leaving it shows a "You are almost done with your first invoice" dialog with the buttons "Yes, I want to leave" / "No, I Want To Stay" and a how-to video link (not clicked).

## Purchase & Expense (sub-menu seen)
Purchase Bills (+), Payment-Out (+), Expenses (+), Purchase Order (+), Purchase Return/ Dr. Note (+). The Purchase Bills empty state says "Make Purchase invoices & Print or share with your customers directly via WhatsApp or Email", with a button "Add Your First Purchase Invoice". Other Income is not in this menu; it is a setting that is off by default (see below).

## Screens NOT opened, and why (updated)
- Sale menu (invoice form, estimate, sale order, delivery challan, credit note, payment-in, POS): clicking Sale always shows the first-sale wizard ("Enter details to make your first Sale"). It never expands, because the app requires a first invoice before the Sale screens open. Getting past it would mean creating an invoice, which this look-only walkthrough did not do.
- Purchase Bill form, Payment-Out, Purchase Order, Purchase Return, Expenses forms: only the menu and the empty Purchase Bills screen were seen.
- Other Income: off in settings, so there is no menu entry.
- Item units, categories and godown screens, and stock adjust: only the Add Item form was seen.
- Party groups and reminders: only seen as settings.
- Report contents: only the report names were recorded.
- Ctrl+F Open Anything: did not open a list.
- Shortcuts key list: blocked by the wizard. Help and Versions menus: the user interrupted before they were read.
- Premium marks seen: the crown on Sync & Share, the trial banner and "Get Vyapar Premium", and "100 CREDITS" on transaction messages.

# Kirana master item list: personal care, household and pooja

## What GST rates apply after the September 2025 (GST 2.0) change?

### Takeaway
Toothpaste, toilet soap, shampoo, hair oil, toothbrushes and shaving cream dropped from 18% to 5% from 22 September 2025. Sources disagree on detergents: the more credible reports say detergents stayed at 18%. Agarbatti is 5%. Sanitary pads are exempt (0%).

### Cited Findings
- GST 2.0 took effect on 22 September 2025 — [Corpzo](https://www.corpzo.com/new-gst-reforms-20)
- Hair oil, shampoo, toothpaste, toilet soap, toothbrushes and shaving cream moved to 5% — [Vyapar](https://vyaparapp.in/blog/new-gst-rates/); [Upstox 5% list](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/tax/new-gst-rates-full-list-of-items-with-5-goods-and-services-tax/article-180697); [Upstox what gets cheaper](https://upstox.com/news/personal-finance/tax/gst-2-0-what-gets-cheaper-and-what-gets-expensive-from-september-22-check-full-list/article-181688)
- Detergents were left out of the rate cut, which the FMCG industry saw as a miss — [Financial Express via Magzter](https://www.magzter.com/en/stories/newspaper/Financial-Express-Kochi/GST-20-DETERGENTS-OUT-OF-RATE-CUT-IN-MISS-FOR-FMCG-FIRMS); [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/stocks/gst-2-0-gst-cuts-elude-these-key-items-analysts-and-fmcg-players-say-it-is-surprising/article-180835). Contradicted by [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/fmcg-gst-rate-list-kirana-store-2026), which lists detergent at 5% but is a less reliable source.
- Agarbatti is 5% — [Vajiram & Ravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/gst-rates-in-india/); [TaxHeal Ch.33](https://www.taxheal.com/gst-rates-chapter-33-essential-oils-and-resinoids-perfumery-cosmetic-or-toilet-preparations.html). One source says 12% — [IndiaFilings](https://www.indiafilings.com/learn/gst-rate-for-perfume-cosmetics-and-toiletries) (outlier, probably out of date).
- Reference for the older CGST rate schedule: [Karnataka CGST rates PDF](https://gst.kar.nic.in/Documents/General/1_CGSTrates.pdf)

### Inferences
- In the app, default detergents, dishwash, floor and toilet cleaners, insecticides, deodorants, batteries and foil to 18%.
- Default toothpaste, soap, shampoo, hair oil, toothbrush, shaving cream and tooth powder to 5%.
- Sanitary pads are 0%. This comes from the July 2018 exemption (my own knowledge, not re-verified in this session).
- HSN codes are standard tariff headings from my own knowledge (3306 oral care, 3401 soap, 3402 detergent, 3305 hair, 3304 skin and talc, 3307 shaving, deo and agarbatti, 3808 insecticides, 9603 brushes, 8506 batteries, 7607 foil, 4818 tissue). They are not checked line by line against CBIC. Use them at the 4-digit level.
- Pack sizes come from my own knowledge of common Indian retail SKUs (as of 2025). They are not checked against each brand's site in this session, and brands change pack sizes often.
- Make GST % editable per item in the app.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch the official CBIC notification (Notification 9/2025-CTR) in this session. These rates are still unconfirmed: detergents, talc, face wash, skin creams, mouthwash, razor blades, candles, matches, camphor, stationery and diapers. They are marked "unsure" or "unknown" in the table.
- I found no sources for brand-specific 2026 pack sizes.

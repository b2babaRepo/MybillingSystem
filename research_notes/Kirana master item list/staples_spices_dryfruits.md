# Kirana master item list: staples, spices, dry fruits (India, 2026)

## Which GST rates apply to kirana staples after the 22 Sept 2025 change?

### Takeaway
Under GST 2.0 (in force from 22 Sept 2025), nearly every kirana staple is now nil or 5%. Ghee, dry fruits, namkeen, pickles and vermicelli dropped to 5%. Spices, sugar and edible oils stay at 5%. Loose cereals, flours and pulses stay nil, and pre-packaged, labelled ones pay 5%.

### Cited Findings
- Ghee, dried nuts and packed namkeen came down to 5% from 12%, effective 22 Sept 2025 — [Petpooja](https://blog.petpooja.com/finance-compliance/new-gst-rates-on-food/); one outlet says the old ghee rate was 18% — [Zeezest](https://zeezest.com/food/revised-gst-rates-on-foods-beverages-in-india-9586)
- Almonds, pistachios, cashews and dates went from 12% to 5% — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/business%2Feconomy%2Frevised-gst-rates-full-list-of-items-that-will-get-cheaper-or-costlier-3711489)
- Sugar (HSN 1701) is 5%. Loose jaggery is exempt, and packaged, labelled jaggery is 5% — [Busy: sugar](https://busy.in/gst-rates/sugar), [Busy: jaggery](https://busy.in/gst-rates/jaggery)
- Whole, crushed and ground spices and simple spice mixtures are 5%. Goods under HSN 2103 moved from 18% to 5% — [Busy: spices](https://busy.in/gst-rates/spices.md)
- Edible oils are in the 5% slab — [GimBooks](https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/new-gst-rates-in-india-2025/amp/)
- The CBIC ready reckoner lists pre-packaged, labelled honey (0409) at 2.5% CGST, which means 5% in total — [CBIC reckoner (mirror)](https://a2ztaxcorp.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/CBIC-GST-Ready-Reckoner-indicating-updated-Central-Goods-and-Services-Tax-CGST-rates-on-goods.pdf)
- HSN-wise list of old and new rates — [myBillBook](https://mybillbook.in/gst-rate-changes-2025-hsn-list-old-vs-new)

### Inferences
- These rates are inferred from the long-standing pre-2025 treatment, carried forward, and were not checked against a source this session: salt nil; loose cereals, atta and dal nil but packed 5%; papad nil; pickles (2001), vermicelli and pasta (1902) and ready mixes (2106) at 5% after the cut. Every row marked "unsure" or "verify" needs checking against CBIC Notification 09/2025-CT(R) dated 17 Sept 2025 before release.
- HSN codes are 4-digit headings taken from standard tariff knowledge. Brand pack sizes are typical retail sizes and were not checked on each brand's site.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed: the 8-digit HSN codes and post-reform rates for sabudana (1903), soya chunks (2106 or 2304), makhana, hing, cornflour, oats, baking powder and soda, and oilseeds used as spices (1207).
- Brand pack sizes come from general market knowledge, and some SKUs may have been discontinued or resized.

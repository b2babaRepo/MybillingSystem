# Other paint brands and paint-shop accessories (seed master list)

Research method note: only a few web lookups were made, using GST pages, aggregator price pages and the Dr. Fixit official page. Product line names and pack sizes come from widely known brand catalogues, but this pass did NOT check them page by page. Every MRP not tied to a source is marked "?". Treat this as a seed list for a shopkeeper to edit, not as verified pricing.

## Brand paint lines (Nerolac, Dulux, Nippon, Indigo, JSW, Shalimar, British Paints)

### Takeaway
About 60 common lines are listed below. Most dealers sell them in the same pack ladder: 0.9/1 L, 3.6/4 L, 9/10 L and 18/20 L for emulsions and enamels, plus 50 ml to 500 ml packs for enamels and stainers. Unit is L (litre) for liquids and kg for putty, POP and cement. HSN is 3209 for water-based paint (emulsion, distemper) and 3208 for solvent-based paint (enamel, PU, varnish). GST is 18% from 22 Sep 2025.

### Cited Findings
- Paints under HSN 3208/3209 moved from 28% to 18% GST effective 22 Sep 2025 — [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/hsn-code-paint); [Gimbooks](https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/paint-hsn-code-3208-gst-rate/amp/); [IBEF](https://ibef.org/news/two-rate-gst-structure-approved-new-rates-to-kick-in-from-september-22-2025-union-minister-for-finance-and-corporate-affairs-ms-nirmala-sitharaman)
- 3209 covers paints and varnishes in an aqueous (water) medium, at 18% — [RegisterKaro](https://www.registerkaro.in/hsn/gst-rate-hsn-code-3209)
- One aggregator shows a 5%/18% split under heading 3208 (Notif. 9/2025-CT(Rate)). This is unconfirmed and conflicts with the other sources — [TaxClue](https://taxclue.in/hsn-code/3208)
- Nerolac 20 L aggregator prices (not official MRP): Impressions Eco Clean Rs 8,103 on one page vs Rs 11,069 on another; Excel Mica Marble Rs 5,507 vs Rs 6,435; Excel Anti Peel about Rs 2,700; Excel about Rs 5,247 — [AapkaPainter](https://aapkapainter.com/products/paints/nerolac-paints-price); [NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/nerolac-paints-price-list/)

## Paint-shop accessories and allied items

### Takeaway
Accessories turn over faster than paint. The key HSN codes are: brushes and rollers 9603, sandpaper 6805, masking tape 3919/4823, thinner/turpentine 3814/3805, white cement 2523, putty 3214, POP 2520, adhesives and M-Seal 3506 (or 3214 for sealant putties), waterproofing 3824/3214, polish 3208/3210, stainers 3212/3204, and spray paint 3208. After Sept 2025, most of these appear to sit at 18%. Brushes and brooms under 9603 may be at 5%, but sources conflict. White cement and POP may also have changed rate.

### Cited Findings
- Adhesives under HSN 3506 are listed at 18% (9% CGST + 9% SGST) — [KnowYourGST](https://www.knowyourgst.com/hsn-gst-tax-rate/adhesives); [RegisterKaro](https://www.registerkaro.in/hsn/gst-rate-hsn-code-3506)
- Abrasive paper/cloth 68053000 is listed at 18%, with a note on rate changes effective 22.09.2025 — [IncorpX](https://www.incorpx.io/hsn-codes/code-68053000); [Busy](https://busy.in/hsn/hsn-68053000.md)
- Heading 9603: the "Brooms, brushes, hand-operated sweepers" line is shown at 5%, but other sub-lines differ, so the rate for paint brushes is unclear — [Busy](https://busy.in/hsn/paint-brush-hsn-code.md); [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/brooms-brushes-floor-sweepers-mops-feather-dusters-gst-rates-hsn-code-9603)
- Dr. Fixit Pidiproof LW+ 200 ml: the official page shows MRP Rs 37, but the same site's pack table shows Rs 47, so the figure conflicts — [Dr. Fixit](https://www.drfixit.co.in/product/new/dr.-fixit-lw--180.html); [AapkaPainter](https://aapkapainter.com/waterproofing/dr-fixit-prices/pidiproof-lw)

### Inferences
- Use 18% as the default GST for every 32xx paint, 3214 putty, 3506 adhesive and 6805 abrasive item. Set 9603 brushes and 2520/2523 POP and white cement as "check with CA" items, because those rate changes are unconfirmed.
- Pack ladder template for the app: emulsion = 1/4/10/20 L; enamel = 50 ml/100 ml/200 ml/500 ml/1 L/4 L; putty = 1/5/20/40 kg; adhesives = g/kg ladder; sandpaper = per sheet by grit (60–1200).
- MRP varies by shade base (white vs deep/clear base) and by state. Dealers sell tinted colours at base MRP plus a tint charge. So the app should let the shopkeeper edit the MRP per pack.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 MRPs from the official sites of Dulux, Nippon, Indigo, JSW, Shalimar or British Paints. Only Nerolac aggregator figures and one Dr. Fixit figure were found.
- The current GST rates for 9603 (paint brushes), 2520 (POP), 2523 (white cement) and 3824 (waterproofing chemicals) after Sept 2025 are not confirmed from a CBIC notification. Check Notification 9/2025-CT(Rate) Schedules I/II.
- Sub-line names (e.g. Beauty Gold, Halo Plus, Weatherbond Pro) and some pack sizes come from general knowledge, not page-by-page verification. Some products may have been renamed or discontinued.
- No MRPs were found for Fevicol SH 1 kg, Raincoat 4 L, M-Seal, Touchwood, Norton sandpaper or Kobra spray.

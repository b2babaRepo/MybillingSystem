## Tata Steel retail brands and product lines

### Takeaway
The retail brands confirmed in Tata Steel reports are Tata Tiscon (TMT rebar), Tata Shaktee (GC roofing sheets), Tata Wiron (galvanised wire), Tata Agrico (hand and agricultural tools), Tata Structura (structural tubes) and Tata Pravesh (steel doors and windows). Durashine is a Tata BlueScope colour-coated sheet brand. Tata nexarc is Tata Steel's B2B and SME buying site.

### Cited Findings
- Tata Shaktee is the galvanised roofing-sheet brand, launched in 2000. Tiscon and Shaktee are named as the main brands. — [Tata Steel AR 2014-15](https://www.tatasteel.com/investors/annual-report-2014-15/html/products-and-brands.html)
- Tata Agrico is Tata Steel's oldest brand and also covers files, pliers, spanners, wrenches and screwdrivers. Tata Wiron makes galvanised wire products. — [Tata Steel IR 2016-17](https://www.tatasteel.com/investors/integrated-report-2016-17/principal-activities-and-revenue-streams.html)
- Tata Pravesh (steel doors with a wood finish) launched in FY2014-15. — [Tata Steel AR 2014-15](https://www.tatasteel.com/investors/annual-report-2014-15/html/products-and-brands.html)
- Tata Structura (tubes) is listed for individual house builders and infrastructure. — [Tata Steel IR 2021-22](https://www.tatasteel.com/investors/integrated-report-2021-22/product-portfolio.html)
- Durashine is a colour-coated sheet brand, described as a Tata BlueScope Steel product. — [MGS Architecture](https://www.mgsarchitecture.in/updates/news/2342-durashine-from-tata-bluescope-steel-is-india-s-most-trusted-brand-2019.html)

### Inferences
- Tata Ezynest and the current Tata Pipes branding (for example "Tata Pipes GI/ERW") were not confirmed in this pass.

### Gaps
- There is no current 2026 brand list from tatasteel.com. Durashine's ownership after the Tata BlueScope restructuring is unconfirmed.

## Indicative prices (2026)

### Takeaway
Tata Tiscon TMT sells at roughly Rs 55-81/kg depending on city and grade. Most sources cluster around Rs 64-74/kg before GST.

### Cited Findings
- Tata Tiscon (Fe500D/550SD): indicative Rs 63-81/kg. — [Comaron](https://www.comaron.com/blog/best-tmt-bar-brand-india-2026-fe500d-comparison-price-quality)
- Tiscon Fe500D, 8-16 mm: about Rs 71-74/kg in Goa and Rs 64-67/kg in Ludhiana and Nashik. These figures are modelled, not observed. — [Infralens](https://infralens.in/prices/steel)
- Chennai TMT (all brands), June 2026: Rs 57-73/kg. — [Tata nexarc](https://www.tatanexarc.com/steel/tmt-bars/tmt-bars-in-tamil-nadu/chennai-price/)
- Vizag: Tiscon at Rs 52,000-55,000 per tonne, which conflicts with the higher figures above. — [Comaron Vizag](https://www.comaron.com/blog/vizag-tmt)

### Gaps
- No sourced prices were found for binding wire, sheets, pipes or tools. Those values in the table are marked "?".

## GST rates after the September 2025 changes

### Takeaway
Cement went from 28% to 18%. Iron and steel goods (Chapters 72 and 73) mostly stay at 18%. Agricultural hand tools (8201) went from 12% to 5%. Workshop hand tools (8203-8205) stay at 18%.

### Cited Findings
- Cement cut from 28% to 18%, effective 22 Sep 2025. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/economy/news/gst-rate-cut-medium-term-gains-cement-firms-125090401223_1.html); [Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/gst-rate-construction-building-materials/)
- Iron and steel rods, wires and similar goods carry a uniform 18%. — [Housing.com](https://housing.com/news/gst-rate-construction-building-materials/). This is contradicted by [Disytax](https://disytax.com/hsn-73-articles-iron-steel-gst-rate/), which claims TMT fell to 5%. The claim looks doubtful and is not corroborated.
- Agricultural hand tools are in the 5% list. Tools for mechanical workshop operations stay at 18%. — [Busy](https://busy.in/gst-rates/construction-materials); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/tax/gst-rates-on-construction-materials-in-2025-gst-on-cement-sand-and-bricks)

### Gaps
- The CBIC notification text (9/2025-CT(Rate)) was not fetched. The rate for asbestos-cement sheets (6811) is unconfirmed: 12% before and possibly 5% now (?).

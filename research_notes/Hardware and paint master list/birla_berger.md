# Birla Opus, Birla White and Berger paint ranges (seed data for paint-shop master item list)

Sources used: Birla Opus's own site (birlaopus.com interiors and exteriors pages), aapkapainter.com (lists Birla Opus 2026 MRPs), NoBroker blogs, IndustryBuying, Moglix, IndiaMART, LnT SuFin, UltraTech's Birla White page, the Berger 2018 MRP PDF, and GST rate sites.
How sure the values are: "?" means unverified. Prices are in ₹ and include GST. Names marked "(src)" were seen in a source. Names without that tag come from model knowledge and should be checked.

## Q1. Birla Opus product names (confirmed from birlaopus.com)

### Takeaway
Birla Opus sells in four tiers. "One" is luxury, split into One True (exterior) and One Pure (interior). Calista is premium, Style is economy and "Dream" is a further line. Other lines: Calista Sparkle for enamels, Allwood for wood finishes, Alldry for waterproofing, and One Pro / Calista Pro / Style Pro for primers and putty. The names in the brief ("Kids", "One Weathershield", "Calista Ever Wash" as an exterior) are partly wrong. Ever Wash is an interior line.

## Q3. Berger range

### Takeaway
Berger's tiers:
- Interior: Silk / Silk Glamor / Silk Breathe Easy (luxury), Easy Clean (premium), Rangoli (regular), Bison emulsion and distemper (economy).
- Exterior: WeatherCoat (Anti Dustt, Long Life, All Guard) and Walmasta.
- Enamel: Luxol (premium), Butterfly (regular), Jadoo (economy).
- Wood: Imperia (premium), Woodkeeper (regular).
- Waterproofing: HomeShield and Dampstop.

## Q4. GST and HSN (after 22 Sept 2025)

### Takeaway
Under GST 2.0, paints (HSN 3208, 3209, 3210), putty (3214) and white cement (2523 21) are all at **18%**, cut from 28% from 22 Sept 2025. Some older tables show putty at 12%, but the more recent and better-supported sources say 18%.

### Cited Findings
- Paints 3208/3209/3210 at 18% after GST 2.0, down from 28%. — [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/hsn-code-paint); [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/paint-hsn-code-and-gst-rate)
- Cement, including white cement (2523 21 00), cut from 28% to 18% from 22 Sept 2025. — [Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/cement-gst-percentage-rate-hsn-code-applicability/); [Munim](https://themunim.com/blog/gst-on-cement/)
- Wall putty 3214 at 18%. busy.in lists 12%, which looks outdated. — [ConstructionPlacements](https://www.constructionplacements.com/gst-rates-on-construction-services-materials-india-sept-2025-update); [busy.in](https://busy.in/gst-rates/paints.md)
- Suggested HSN for each category:
  - 3209: water-based emulsions, distemper, primers and waterproofing coats.
  - 3208: solvent-based enamels, PU wood finishes and red oxide primer.
  - 3214: putty.
  - 9603: brushes and rollers (18%?).
  - 2523 21: white cement.
  - Source: model knowledge plus the sources above.

### Gaps
- The CBIC notification text was not fetched directly.

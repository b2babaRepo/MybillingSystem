## Source quality notes

- Aapka Painter's emulsion page contradicts itself: its FAQ gives Rs 1,500-1,800 for 20 L Apex Ultima, but its list gives 12,710. The page also says it does not sell the products. — [AP-EMU](https://aapkapainter.com/paints/asian-paints-prices/emulsion)

- For seed data, ship the price as an editable default and flag it "verify with dealer price list".

- None beyond those above.

## GST rate and HSN codes after the Sept 2025 GST 2.0 change

### Takeaway
Paints and varnishes (HSN 3208, 3209, 3210) are taxed at 18% GST, and this was unchanged in the 22 Sept 2025 rationalisation (they moved from 28% to 18% back in 2018). Putty (3214) is generally 18%. Brushes (9603), thinner (3814) and adhesives (3506) are 18% from trade knowledge but were not verified from a primary source.

### Cited Findings
- 3208 and 3209 paints are at 18%, unchanged through the revision effective 22 Sept 2025, and taxed at 18% since 2018. — [Busy](https://busy.in/gst-rates/paints.md); [Cleartax](https://cleartax.in/s/paint-hsn-code-and-gst-rate)
- Paint HSN 3208/3209/3210 is at 18% in 2026, and wall putty 3214 is at 18%. — [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/hsn-code-paint)
- 3214 is listed at 18% overall, but one lookup shows 32141000 as 5% or 18% depending on the goods. — [RegisterKaro](https://www.registerkaro.in/hsn/gst-rate-hsn-code-3214); [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/hsn-code/32141000)

### Inferences
- Default HSN by category: emulsion/distemper/water primer/waterproof coating 3209; enamel/solvent primer/PU/melamine 3208; other varnish, distemper or dry pigment 3210; putty/fillers 3214; stainers 3204/3212; thinner/MTO 3814; adhesive 3506; brushes/rollers 9603; sandpaper 6805; masking tape 3919; scrapers 8205. All are 18%.

### Gaps
- The CBIC notification 9/2025-CT(Rate) text was not checked directly. The 3814, 3506, 9603 and 6805 rates are unverified, so mark them "?" until confirmed.

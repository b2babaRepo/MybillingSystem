3. **Your records.** You own your data. You are responsible for entries being correct and for customers' consent to be

4. **Orders.** Prices and availability are set by the shop. The contract for goods is between the household and the shop.
5. **AI-read lists.** Lists read from a note, photo, voice message or WhatsApp message are suggestions and can be wrong. Check

   that nobody corrected.
6. **New shops and blocking.** We may review new shops before households can find them. We may block a shop that is fake,

7. **Fair use.** No spam reminders, no scraping, no repeated requests for sign-in codes, and no attempts to reach other shops'
   data.
8. **Availability.** We aim for the app to work offline and to sync later, but we don't guarantee uninterrupted service.

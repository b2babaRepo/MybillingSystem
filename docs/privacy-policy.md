# Privacy policy (DRAFT: needs review by a lawyer for India's DPDP Act 2023 before launch)

Grocery Khata ("we") runs a billing and khata app for kirana shops, and a list app for households.

**What we hold.** For shops: shop name, area, UPI ID, staff names and phone numbers. Also what the shop records: products,
bills, and customers' names and phone numbers with their khata (credit) entries. For households: name, phone number, address,
grocery lists and orders. We don't collect location tracking, contacts or payment card data.

**Whose data it is.** A shop's customer records belong to the shop. We process them on the shop's behalf; the shop is
responsible for having its customers' consent. A household's lists belong to the household. A shop sees only the orders sent
to it.

**What we use it for.** Only to run the app: bills, balances, reminders that the shop chooses to send, and order status. We
don't sell data or show ads, and we don't share one shop's data with another.

**Sign-in codes by SMS.** When you sign in with a code, your phone number and the code go to our SMS provider, MSG91 (India),
only to deliver that message. We keep the code only as a scrambled (hashed) value, for at most a day.

**Reading lists with AI (only if you agree).** A household can write a note, speak, or add a photo of a list, and have it read
into list items. This happens only after you agree in the app, and we record when you agreed.
- A typed note or a photo is sent to Anthropic (the Claude AI service) to be read. [Lawyer: Anthropic processes data outside
  India; confirm the cross-border transfer wording and that Anthropic does not train on this data under our agreement.]
- A voice note is sent to Sarvam AI (India) to be turned into text, then read the same way.
- We never store the photo or the recording. We keep what you typed and the items the AI suggested for 30 days, to check and
  improve accuracy, and then delete them.
- You can withdraw at any time in the app. That deletes all your AI records at once.

**Orders by WhatsApp (only if you agree).** If you message Grocery Khata's WhatsApp number, Meta (WhatsApp) delivers the
message to us. We read it the same way as above, and only for people who have an account and have agreed to AI reading.
Anyone else gets a short reply saying how to join; their message isn't read or kept.

**Who can see your data at Grocery Khata.** A small number of our staff (platform admins) can see each shop's name, area, its
owner's and staff's names and phone numbers, and how many customers and bills it has, to approve shops, block misuse and give
support. They don't browse a shop's customers or khata entries. They may keep a private support note about a shop.

**Where it is kept.** On servers in India, encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Backups are encrypted and kept for 14 days. The
services above (MSG91, Anthropic, Sarvam, Meta) receive only what is needed for their one task.

**Your rights.** A shop owner can download all of the shop's data, or delete the shop, from Settings. A household can delete
its account, and can withdraw AI consent at any time. Customers of a shop should contact the shop first; they can also contact
us at the grievance address below.

**Breaches.** If personal data leaks, we will tell affected shops and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Act requires.
